Maps, Layers and Tensors — prioritized data-gap plan

Planning review, 25 October 2026. Source: all 22 saved entries in the live atlas's shared review feed, including all 12 nonempty notes. The read-only snapshot retains the original wording, stable keys and versions. Shared owner flags have not been changed. No extraction repair, tensor rebuild or training was performed by making this plan. All tasks below remain open.

The existing extraction work queue remains the task register. The order below is a recommended sequence, not a promise that every item is equally quick. Dependent measurements should not be recomputed across the library before their definitions are settled.

A. Quick, well-defined work

  • EXTRACT-05 — Separate Terrain and Slope views. Show elevation and slope as independent fields, with documented units and a shared cross-map scale. Use numeric source heights/slope, not shading from the vault picture. Preserve the accepted pathing thresholds. This is primarily a presentation addition; it does not by itself introduce a newly validated tensor channel.
  • EXTRACT-06a — Show true map/playable dimensions and hide unplayable padding. Label cases such as DualGap's nonstandard playable footprint explicitly and mask outside the serialized playable rectangle. Do not render unused space as flat usable terrain. Keep native-file dimensions separate from playable size. Auditing numeric measurements and tensor losses against those bounds is the separate, more substantial EXTRACT-06b follow-up below.
  • EXTRACT-14 — Make an exact-version scenario/coverage checklist. For each of the five atlas maps, list the map key/version, active slots, teams, movement profile, adaptive configuration, analysis method/release and coordinate bounds for every derived layer. Distinguish missing output, known-wrong scenario and merely missing display. This makes the DualGap mismatch and missing Setons FAF/FAF Beetles outputs actionable without silently substituting stock maps.

B. Bounded implementation and validation

  • EXTRACT-01 — Repair the known DualGap air scenario. Use ARMY_1 versus ARMY_2 for 8070_26498, verify against source starts, then regenerate expansion, offensive-drop and raid outputs in a new release. The historical ARMY_12 masks remain history. Moving/relabeling a preview is not the repair. General symmetry and opportunity-ranking improvements are separate tasks.
  • EXTRACT-06b — Propagate playable bounds into actual data use. Check array registration, coordinate conversion, water/area denominators, route searches and learning masks. Add/repair explicit validity masks where padding currently contributes. Rebuild only affected datasets after the audit identifies them; this is not completed by making the atlas background transparent.
  • EXTRACT-09 — Unify the access presentation and add missing access classes. Bring Walk access and Access structure into one coherent section. Keep their underlying distance/component fields distinct. Expose land access, extra hover access and genuinely drop-only standable/landing areas. A cell is not a valid drop destination merely because walking cannot reach it. Retain per-side identity and the accepted movement assumptions. Depends on a correct scenario; global symmetry enforcement follows the agreed EXTRACT-07 policy.
  • EXTRACT-02 — Fill movement-profile and exact-version coverage. Locate the saved hover evidence, identify genuinely missing navy evidence, and fill the missing newer frontier inputs for FAF Beetles 10838_21065. Fill missing exact-version source/whole-economy coverage for Setons FAF 8553_17709 and FAF Beetles when the applicable definitions are fixed. Compare like methods and scenarios; old hover routes are not automatically newer frontier results, and amphibious routes are not navy. New connected-route calculations depend on EXTRACT-13; new zoning outputs depend on EXTRACT-03/04.
  • EXTRACT-12a — Inventory appearance records before choosing a learning target. Locate serialized clouds/cirrus, skybox settings, declared static effects and map-script effect references. Keep them separate from painted materials, decals and the lit preview. Report static, time-dependent and unresolved cases. This is the bounded first step toward the requested appearance layers; a complete effect renderer/runtime extractor is not assumed to already exist.

C. More difficult work requiring explicit rules

  • EXTRACT-07 — One symmetry contract for every derived measurement. Determine and display the relevant transform: vertical/horizontal/diagonal or angled reflection, 180-degree rotation, another supported transform, or unresolved. Use the same accepted transform, active slots and coordinate frame throughout access, routes, economy zones and all air analyses, rather than mirroring only the picture. Decide: tolerances for approximately symmetric maps, how to resolve terrain/resource/slot disagreements, treatment of asymmetric maps or configurations, and how enforced analysis symmetry relates to preserved raw source observations. A symmetric input/scenario must produce correspondingly symmetric results; material source data is not silently rewritten as evidence.
  • EXTRACT-13 — Turn frontier approaches into valid complete battle paths. Paths must reach the opposing base through actual passable connections, with shared trunks/branches explicit. Current K3 frontier approaches can end at separate contacts; simply joining their endpoints can invent an impassable crossing. Decide: how compatible contacts are paired/connected, which alternate passages deserve a distinct path, and how disconnected movement layers are represented. Keep the distinction between a frontier contact, a route witness and an independent attack lane. Validate continuity numerically on the selected source grid; a continuous-looking line is insufficient.
  • EXTRACT-03 + EXTRACT-04 — Rework economy zoning and contestability together. Merge the confusing presentation, then agree what main base, rear/safe economy, own expansion, contestable region and active fight/hot zone actually mean. Decide: whether location, ownership, access and exposure are separate axes or mutually exclusive classes, and which response/arrival assumptions define contestability. Give mexes and reclaim separate selectable layers; reclaim is finite and collection-dependent, while mexes provide continuing income. Do not assume every forward resource is contested or every rear resource safe. Every marker/value allocation must reconcile with its source records and chosen scenario. Depends on corrected slots, bounds, access, symmetry and routes.
  • EXTRACT-08 — Rank air opportunities, rather than marking every spot equally. Add numerical good/better opportunity scores to expansion drops, offensive drops and raids, preserving the component evidence. Decide: resource benefit, engineer travel savings/payback, route/base response, army dispersion and assumed defense strength; also whether scores are map-relative rankings, cross-map comparable values, or both. Symmetry-equivalent opportunities should receive equal scores under equivalent inputs. Do not tune a formula solely to reproduce named-map expectations. Calibrate on the corrected five-map scenarios before a collection-wide rerun. Actual defenses/intel are not observed from a source map and must remain explicit assumptions.
  • EXTRACT-10 — Define common slot/team layouts by game mode. Establish intended configurations for 1v1, 2v2, 3v3, 4v4 and 6v6, including mirrored counterparts, team membership and ambiguous-map overrides. Decide: which layouts are canonical/common, when map metadata is sufficient, and how team response/resource denominators differ from the current 1v1 assumptions. Do not treat all possible slot combinations as equally typical or automatically relabel a 1v1 calculation as team-game evidence.
  • EXTRACT-11 — Resolve adaptive props, wrecks and civilians. Extend beyond spawn-to-mex/hydro links to option- and slot-dependent entity groups, presence, quantity and low/medium/high reclaim/civilian settings. First verify the saved default active configuration; keep declared associations, global multipliers and runtime-created entities distinct. Decide: which script patterns can be supported statically and where controlled runtime observation is necessary. Do not infer ownership from proximity or enumerate every setting combination before defining the supported scope. Preserve entity identity and mass/energy accounting when options alter the active population.
  • EXTRACT-12b — Decide how clouds/effects belong in the representation. After the inventory, choose separate parameters/event records, separate spatial layers, or a fixed-time rendered appearance target. Define the time/camera and behavior of animated effects if rendering is chosen. Do not bake weather or lighting into the material-weight tensor by default or call a preview-only effect a completed scientific extraction.
