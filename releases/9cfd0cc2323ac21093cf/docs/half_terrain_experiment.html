Half-Map Terrain Experiment

Generate a north half at 64x128 cells, then rotate it 180 degrees to construct the south half. Height, signed water boundary and depth are generated simultaneously. This is rotational symmetry, not a reflection across a horizontal axis.

The full-map model and its existing results remain untouched. No resources, props, textures or playable FAF export are part of this experiment.

Data

The audited joint-terrain dataset is the source. Select compatible maps using fixed, approximate thresholds: encoded height rotational MAE <= 0.04, water-mask disagreement <= 3%, and opposite-spawn displacement <= 12 world units. This is not a quality classification. Some good asymmetric maps are intentionally excluded.

Only train and validation manifests are read. Their original family splits and train-fitted normalization are preserved. Test data stays at the source and is not inspected. Archives are referenced by absolute path and verified by SHA-256, not copied or modified. The selection records accepted and rejected maps.

Training rotates/flips full source arrays, then takes the north half. Thus each batch remains 64x128 while augmentation can expose either source half. Two source spawns are adjusted to an exactly opposite pair by their midpoint discrepancy; their combined Gaussian field is cropped to one half as a single input channel. Spawns are input anchors, not generated predictions.

Water fraction and relief controls are recomputed for the selected half, whose rotated completion has the same statistics. Legacy economic controls are masked out because they need not describe that completion. Source terrain is not averaged or otherwise forced into symmetry before cropping.

Join And Diagnostics

Raw assembly copies model predictions exactly and preserves their water fraction. It guarantees 180-degree raster symmetry but not a smooth central join. A separate optional decoder linearly interpolates four rows on each side of the join: an eight-cell / 32-world-unit band. It operates on all three physical fields. The repaired signed-distance field is an approximation within that band.

A third view reconciles height with water boundary and depth using the existing joint projection. Raw, repaired and projected arrays/previews remain separate. Metrics report central height jumps, interior height differences, water-mask changes, correction magnitude, approximate connectivity and symmetry error. Native 513x513 vertices explicitly handle their odd centre row to preserve symmetry. These structural checks do not establish in-game balance or pathability.

Windows Commands

$env:PYTHONPATH = 'src'
.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m supmapai.ml.half_terrain prepare `
  --source data/tensors/terrain_joint_10km_1v1 `
  --out data/tensors/terrain_half_10km_1v1_v1
.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m supmapai.ml.half_terrain train `
  --dataset data/tensors/terrain_half_10km_1v1_v1 `
  --out data/models/terrain_half_10km_1v1_v1 `
  --max-steps 12000 --batch-size 16 --base-channels 48 `
  --eval-every 1000 --sample-every 2000

The run trains from scratch with the existing U-Net, velocity objective, EMA, bfloat16, family-weighted sampling and checkpoint/resume machinery. It does not reuse full-map weights. Loss values are not directly comparable across different cohorts and tensor shapes. Periodic previews use seeds 101/202; the final gallery adds 303/404. Each seed is tested with requested water fractions 0%, 20%, 40%.

Check progress.json while training; last.pt allows --resume with the same configuration and code. Training outputs and datasets remain gitignored.

Fresh galleries from an existing checkpoint (use a new output folder):

.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m supmapai.tools.sample_half_terrain `
  --checkpoint data/models/terrain_half_10km_1v1_v1/best.pt `
  --out data/models/terrain_half_10km_1v1_v1/fresh_samples `
  --seeds 505 606 707 808
.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m supmapai.tools.evaluate_half_terrain `
  --checkpoint data/models/terrain_half_10km_1v1_v1/best.pt `
  --dataset data/tensors/terrain_half_10km_1v1_v1 `
  --out data/models/terrain_half_10km_1v1_v1/validation_diagnostics
.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m supmapai.tools.report_half_terrain `
  --run data/models/terrain_half_10km_1v1_v1 `
  --dataset data/tensors/terrain_half_10km_1v1_v1

These are the commands used for the completed pilot. Existing sample folders are preserved: choose a different --out for another sampling run.

Pilot Results

Completed 12,000 updates on 1,200 training maps / 110 validation maps in 463.6 seconds (7.7 minutes), excluding initial data loading. Peak allocated training GPU memory was 970.5 MiB. The model has 2,986,307 parameters. Best EMA validation objective was 0.013371 at the final update; it is not a gameplay-quality metric.

Generated 24 final samples across eight seeds and three requested water settings:

Requested water Raw assembled mean Join-repaired mean
0% 0.2% 0.2%
20% 16.3% 16.2%
40% 45.0% 44.9%

All 24 archives passed explicit finite-value, half-shape, bit-exact rotational symmetry and water-area-preservation checks. Symmetry error is zero in raw, repaired, projected and native 513x513 fields. This is guaranteed by assembly, not evidence that the network independently learned full-map symmetry.

The original four-seed final gallery illustrates the remaining join problem: mean raw central height jump 6.315 world units, reduced to 0.651 by the narrow repair. Mean repair height correction over the whole map was 0.137 world units; at most 1.31% of water-mask cells changed in an individual sample. Reference validation sources average 0.4958 world units across a copied-half join. These are adjacent-row differences on the model grid, not native game slope limits.

Approximate base-to-base land connectivity in that 12-sample gallery was 10/12 raw and 9/12 after repair/projection. Repair is therefore not a playability guarantee and can damage a route. The raw central discontinuity remains a real model limitation; interpolation does not mean the model learned a good join.

Held-out one-pass reconstruction across all 110 validation halves achieved mean height error 0.963 world units and wet-map water IoU 0.925 at timestep 100; at timestep 500 these were 2.443 and 0.787. These are noised-source denoising checks, not fresh-generation scores. Seventeen focused unit tests passed, and reloading the completed run with --resume returned its saved summary without retraining.

Outputs are under data/models/terrain_half_10km_1v1_v1/:

  • half_terrain_report.md and .json: metrics and caveats.
  • samples/step_012000/: four primary seeds, raw/repaired/projected previews and arrays.
  • fresh_samples/: four additional seeds with checkpoint provenance.
  • validation_diagnostics/: held-out reconstruction checks.
  • validation_source_half_comparison.png: original source versus copied-half reference.

Conclusion: half-map generation solves exact terrain symmetry without whole-map averaging, but does not by itself solve the central boundary or water calibration. Keep the full-map model as the baseline. The next half-model improvement should target compatible central boundaries, rather than treating larger smoothing bands as evidence of better generation. No playable FAF map was exported here.

The next dataset version has been relabeled with direct terrain controls; see the relabeling run and training handoff. This changes the conditioning architecture from 13 to 21 inputs. The results above remain a record of the original model and are not results from the new labels.
