Method and ratings

  • Slot 1 versus its 180-degree mirrored slot by preference; if necessary accept a small positional deviation (up to 5% of map width), a matching reflection axis/diagonal, or the only other slot on an asymmetric two-player map. Record the exact choice. Triangular/five-way and survival layouts without a meaningful mirror are excluded rather than assigned an arbitrary opponent.
  • Default adaptive options only; resolve resources for the selected two slots. Unknown resource scripts are not scored from every possible saved marker.
  • Existing terrain movement rules: slope .75; amphibious maximum depth 25; hover engineers can unload on water. All three route layers retain shared sections and enforced approximate symmetry where supported.
  • Same medium primary preset, base response strength 1, automatic land-route strength from connecting-land economic share times route directness. Generic air raids include underwater mexes via torpedo aircraft, with representative gunship economics for simplicity, as explicitly requested by the user.
  • Scores remain geometric opportunity estimates. No actual army placement, player intel/AA, combat damage or map-specific force allocation is claimed.
  • Primary ranking evidence: unique affected mex share, distinct destinations, route counts, widths and shared sections. Preserve the earlier absolute 1-5 formula for reproducibility. New relative 1-5 ratings are midrank percentiles of affected mex share among completed maps, with zero always 1. They are provisional until the cohort finishes, and are not calibrated universal gameplay labels.
  • Land front: best sustained narrowest width among found routes. Closed <24 map units; mixed 24-64; open >=64. Disconnected land is a separate class. Route count is not the number of independent fronts; search budgets make counts lower bounds.
  • Do not tune per-map presets or thresholds to make a desired ranking. The user approved the four-map relative ordering, not an absolute score calibration.
