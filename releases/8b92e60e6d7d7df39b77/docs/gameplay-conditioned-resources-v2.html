Gameplay-conditioned resources: proposed V2 experiment

Status: design only. No new tensor release, checkpoint or training run exists. The selected terrain, marker and reclaim models remain unchanged.

Why this is worth testing

The current marker diffusion has terrain, bases and source mex/hydro counts, but no requested exposure, drop opportunity or economic-front style. Current reclaim allocation models have terrain/bases/markers, but no scalar gameplay profile. They can learn correlations implicit in those inputs; they cannot distinguish two explicit style requests with identical inputs. Missing conditioning is a plausible contributor to generic allocation, not a demonstrated sole cause. Dataset mixture, deterministic prediction, objectives and limited training are alternative factors.

At fixed 10km and 1v1, mex count already determines mexes per player and overall area density. A second name or 1-5 score derived from that same count adds no new information. Exposure and spatial economic distribution are the useful additions.

Three kinds of map information

  1. Physical layers: terrain/water, textures, marker coordinates, props and units.
  2. Derived observations: access, path distances, opportunities, economic fronts, raw supply/handling values, coarse ratings and confidence/validity flags.
  3. Desired controls: requested properties supplied to the generator. Derived observations can supervise their meaning, but are not automatically available as spatial input channels on a new, unfinished map.

Derived properties are consequences of interacting physical layers, not additional independent pieces to serialize into SCMAP. The scientific scoring policy is 1v1; its land/air/naval shares overlap and do not sum to one. Energy is not scored.

Proposed data contract

Keep the existing spatial conditions and clean targets, with new arrays for a small normalized gameplay_profile and a separate gameplay_valid mask. Retain raw values, rating values, confidence, source/version, selected slots and policy hash as metadata. Fit normalization and control presets on retained training families only; reuse the existing family-separated validation set. Missing is not zero.

Initial candidate axes, subject to coverage and correlation audits:

Do not feed every correlated raw scalar and its binned rating as separate equally weighted features. Prefer continuous values for learning, with coarse ratings as an interpretable interface and audit. Do not silently treat ordinal human scores as equivalent to automated ratings; their definitions, configurations and coverage differ. A small profile with reliable variation may work better than all fields.

Prevent circular or unavailable inputs

Source-derived scalar scores can be legitimate training conditions: training uses the source's measured profile, generation uses a user-requested profile, and the completed output is measured again. This is not inherently target leakage.

However, current economic fronts and air/drop opportunity masks depend on source resources, response assumptions and sometimes reclaim. Supplying those exact source-only spatial masks while training and having no equivalent at generation would create an invalid contract or reveal target layout. For each proposed field:

  • If computable from generated terrain and planned bases alone, recompute it at generation time with the same algorithm and alignment.
  • If computed from already generated markers, only use it downstream of markers.
  • If dependent on resources currently being predicted or future layers, use it as a requested scalar/explicit user spatial plan, an auxiliary target, or an audit; do not pretend it is a free terrain feature.

A desired profile does not guarantee feasibility: fixed terrain limits possible fronts, naval reach and access. Flag incompatible requests rather than promising that resource placement can manufacture a missing passage.

Training and evaluation sequence

  1. Join metadata by exact map/version and active two-slot configuration. Report coverage, unknown values, censored fronts, policy versions, duplicates and correlations on the current curated cohort, not the whole scored collection.
  2. Freeze a new dataset release, leaving existing tensors/checkpoints intact. Begin with scalar profile conditioning; add spatial derived features only when an inference-time recipe exists. Check spatial transforms/base swaps explicitly.
  3. Train matched-budget baseline vs gameplay-conditioned marker models with the same splits and seeds. Keep terrain fixed to isolate placement improvements.
  4. Add gameplay conditioning to natural reclaim in a separate experiment. Budgets still scale fixed allocation maps without resampling. If amount is tested as a neural condition, hold that profile fixed during composer-only amount sweeps.
  5. Compare low/medium/high requests on the same terrain, anchors and sampling seed. Measure actual marker counts, early access and raid/drop/front properties after composition. Some properties need the complete mex/reclaim layout to be valid.
  6. Compare source agreement, conditioning response, diversity and expert review. A sharp picture or a lower loss does not establish successful steering. Report unreachable requests and uncertainty. Do not optimize known heuristic errors.

Reclaim abundance scores account for entity handling and mass, not tree energy. Composer budgets and asset/chunk choices can change the measured score even with an unchanged allocation field; raw mass is not interchangeable with this score. The initial experiment need not solve this entire coupled system at once.

Architecture

Suggested first implementation: encode the small scalar profile and inject it into the resource network's existing conditioning embedding; test feature-wise modulation for the deterministic allocation network. This is an experiment, not an established improvement for this dataset. Keep condition dropout/missingness explicit and evaluate whether the network ignores individual conditions.

General conditioning mechanisms: FiLM and classifier-free diffusion guidance. Neither paper establishes that our particular heuristic ratings improve maps.
