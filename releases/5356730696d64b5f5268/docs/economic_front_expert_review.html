What the current percentage measures

The denominator is weighted nearest-base walking distance: sum(resource income × base response distance). Removal value is the increase in that walking-access cost when a complete trace is removed and other traces are held fixed. It is not a share of map mass or a direct measure of strategic importance. Cleanup can preserve the aggregate objective while changing individual trace removal values. Therefore the percentage should remain an inspection metric, not a universal corridor-worth test.

Proposed order of improvements

  1. Stabilize corridor identity before economic selection. Retain legal branch traces and shared trunks, group reviewed small-obstacle detours, and score joint corridor removal. A mirrored pair alone or a low individual score must not cause automatic grouping. Repair Project Dust crossings independently.
  2. Add numerical spatial reclaim to route attraction and evaluation, preserving entity mass and positions, including valid initial wrecks, and resolving the same active slots/default options. Keep live civilian units and unknown runtime entities separate. Do not recover values from PNG colours or treat unknown mass as zero. Cluster/pool the value-preserving grids for tractable route searches. Rear reclaim should remain behind-base economy; avoid restoring endpoint loops.
  3. Compare mex income and finite reclaim in common units: for a shared analysis horizonT, use mex income×T plus available collectible reclaim mass. Include travel/collection constraints and compare early versus sustained horizons. Reclaim is a finite stock, not recurring mex income; raw sums of mass and mass/second are invalid. Do not choose a different horizon per map to force the expected route count.
  4. Evaluate complete corridor configurations across budgets rather than permanently stopping on the first weak trace. Diagnose total service gain, distinct resource/reclaim groups, bypass/attack approaches and geometric independence. Expose core and secondary fronts, ranges and sensitivity where appropriate. Retain old cutoffs for comparison until the objective and grouping are validated. A failure atK4 is not a proof that a reorganizedK5 configuration is worthless.
  5. Recheck the accepted maps as regression controls and the six disagreement cases as diagnostics; then evaluate fresh maps before claiming better generalization. Use the requested Badlandsv4. Keep Sentina III/Millennium excluded. Extend Forbidden Pass to hover/amphibious as separate movement networks, preserving MaxSlope0.75 and amphibious maximum depth25; do not mix those counts into land fronts.
