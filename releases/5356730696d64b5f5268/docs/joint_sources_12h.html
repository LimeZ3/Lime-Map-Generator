What the pilot established

The full pilot completed 2,074 updates in 2,502 seconds: 49,776 sampled map presentations, equivalent to 63.7 cohort passes. All 13 channel validation losses improved. Mean fixed-timestep held-out loss fell from 1.01603 to 0.02745; it was still improving at the end (0.03071 at update 1,514). Median measured pilot GPU utilization was 98%, with mean 94.25% across 156 telemetry samples including loading, validation and sampling.

Fresh-noise outputs now have more localized resource peaks and coherent surface color, while height and RGB remain blurry. Absolute quantities, starting layouts, reclaim amounts and independent rating calibration are not established. For one matched seed, requesting mex rating 1 versus 5 increased the integrated mex field from 442.7 to 703.5 (+58.9%). This is a conditional response, not proof that those values are correct or that all 12 ratings are independently controllable.

Small positive background errors are important: the final source-count fields integrate to hundreds despite only a few strong start peaks. Integrals are not decoded entity counts. The water field has a shallow background bias (median depth about 0.12 world units); the reported wet fraction changes strongly between 0.05 and 0.25 depth thresholds. This sensitivity is documented rather than repaired. Source heatmaps preserve absolute quantities; generated quantity calibration still fails. No playable game export or player test was performed.

The pilot review contains fixed-scale, same-seed comparisons, per-channel curves and numeric methods. Its arrays and checkpoints are preserved unchanged.

The parent is the final pilot last.pt, update 2,074, SHA-256 3ef78a2b12c7b71d7ba8592df30ec7e669070903613376e656a69b1064b416ed. Its verified copy is resumed with model, EMA, AdamW optimizer, random states and history. Initial resumed validation reproduced the parent's value exactly.

Architecture, source representation, complete-case cohort/family split, loss, augmentation, precision and learning rate are unchanged. This isolates a longer compute budget rather than switching to another staged approach. The source contract remains the 13-channel pilot contract:

  • 73,323,149-parameter U-Net, base 128, attention at 32/16 cells.
  • 851 maps: 782 train / 69 validation families; no test data used.
  • Height, physical depth, two starts, mex/hydro, natural energy/mass, initial wreck mass, civilian count density and surface RGB at 256 squared.
  • Generation receives noise, timestep and 12 ratings; zero source spatial conditions, analytical layers or validity masks.
  • Microbatch six, four accumulated microsteps, effective batch 24; bfloat16 compute with float32 parameters/optimizer; learning rate 0.0002.
  • Checkpoints at least every two minutes, held-out validation every 15 minutes, raw fresh-noise galleries every 30 minutes and at completion.
  • Same matched seeds/profiles/sampling settings as the pilot. Numeric sampling bounds and unbounded final estimates remain separately saved. No geometry or placement repair, symmetry projection, count caps, budgets or asset composition.

At the pilot's observed throughput, twelve additional hours should allow roughly 35,000 additional updates, subject to evaluation overhead. This is an estimate, not a fixed update promise. New telemetry samples showed 98% GPU utilization and approximately 15 GB total device memory use after the resume.

Supervision and review

tools/run_joint_sources_long.py enforces the twelve-hour wall-clock budget, checks the inherited source/dataset hashes and checkpoint copy, and records its launch command and parent lineage. It requests cooperative checkpointing before the deadline. Failures, nonfinite training values and ten-minute progress stalls stop the run and preserve completed checkpoints. Windows idle sleep is inhibited only while the supervisor is active; the display can sleep. It does not restart silently, reset the budget, or modify the persistent power plan.
