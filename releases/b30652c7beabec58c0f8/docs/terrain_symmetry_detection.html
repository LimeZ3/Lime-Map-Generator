Terrain-first symmetry extraction - extracted map-data feature

The owner confirmed that editor symmetry selection does not survive ordinary map export. Explicit symmetry metadata searching is skipped. The detector compares native physical terrain against transformed samples; entities are independent corroborating evidence, not substitutes for terrain.

Method

  1. Verify each pinned native NPZ hash, metadata identity and original family/split. Read native physical height_vertices, playable bounds, all source start markers and the already resolved configuration's mexes. These maps have 513×513 native vertices; no enforced-symmetry or preview image is used.
  2. Sample a regular 40×40 equal-area grid at cell centers. Scan reflection axes every 3 degrees from 0 to 180. Refine promising local minima in angle and perpendicular offset. Check rotational orders 2 through 16; refine centers within ±2.5% of the domain extent. This is bounded discovery, not every possible rotation center or local symmetry.
  3. Verify candidates on an independent denser 161×161 grid with bilinear native height samples. Rotation acceptance requires every nonidentity power of that order to pass, not merely the sector generator. A combined rotation and reflection class must verify the whole generated reflection set.
  4. Fit error is height RMSE divided by robust relief (95th minus 5th height percentiles), weighted to keep featureless backgrounds from dominating. Candidate limits: weighted normalized RMSE ≤0.055, normalized absolute-error p95 ≤0.12, continuous domain overlap ≥72%. These are detector tolerances awaiting per-map review, not calibrated probabilities. Retain residuals and all tested candidates.
  5. A whole-domain conditioning candidate additionally requires ≥99.5% domain overlap for every selected transform and no unrepresented accepted mirror. Continuous rectangle intersection avoids a sample-grid endpoint artifact. Negligible terrain variation produces unknown, not all symmetry types.
  6. Transform original start/default-mex positions and measure nearest-source agreement at 3 world-unit tolerance, including unique-target counts. Record every non-self source-slot correspondence. Do not rename/move slots or force a single opposing base when multiple valid mates exist.

Angles are measured from +world-x toward +world-z, in physical coordinates. Mirrors have an axis; rotations have a center and order. One arbitrary class ID would lose these distinctions. For mirror-only maps, the canonical center is the projection of the domain center onto the fitted axis.

Saved data and model role

Each map has result.json (descriptor, expanded transforms, fit errors, separate entity evidence and source pins), symmetry.npz, preview.png, and a hash seal. conditions.npz holds keys, condition (1310, 28), availability and cohort identity. A per-map condition is:

Indices Representation
0–3 One-hot: none, reflection, rotation, rotation_with_reflection
4–19 Rotational order 1–16, one-hot; 1 is identity rotation only
20–21 Center normalized to −1…1 within the playable domain
22–23 [cos(2*n*theta), sin(2*n*theta)] for the mirror-axis set
24–27 Availability of family, order, center and mirror orientation

Unknown/provisional conditions are zero-filled with availability false. A bounded “no match” result has an explicit none category, not the unknown vector. Expanded transform arrays have shape (T, 3, 3), world affine matrices.

Validation and remaining limits

Ten tests cover source-pinned extraction, flat unknowns, asymmetric noise, a half-turn without mirrors, multiple mirror axes, angled/offset axes including angle wraparound, three-sector structure and partial-domain coverage. Independent collection validation checks all seals, implementation snapshots, native/metadata/ marker hashes, source identity/splits, finite vectors, encoding, NPZ shapes and rigid-transform fixed centers. It passed for all 1,310 maps; see integrity.json.

  • Sampling can miss small asymmetric features. This is approximate terrain similarity, not a pixel-perfect certificate; fine-grid agreement is evidence.
  • Robust normalization and thresholds still need review against diverse maps. Uniform terrain cannot identify author intent; entity evidence may help later.
  • Orders above 16, strongly offset centers and exhaustive local symmetry are outside this search. 11097_21654 reaches the order ceiling; don't call its fitted order a proven global maximum. Near-radial terrain can fit many orders.
  • Arbitrary-angle mirrors and radial rotations may only agree on the overlap of a rectangular domain. Corners are not silently discarded from a full-map label. Resolving a meaningful radial playable-domain mask is future work.
  • Height agreement does not certify slope, textures, water enablement, reclaim, starts or every adaptive setting. Current checks cover starts/default mexes; no whole-dataset props or settings-variant symmetry is certified.
  • Source-start matching is a candidate relation, not an automatic 1v1 selection policy. EXTRACT-01 still needs selection rules and downstream scenario repair.
  • A requested model condition encourages symmetry; exact enforcement/validation remains a separate operation. The new fields are not yet trained conditions.

Human-readable labels and geometry masks

data/processed/terrain_symmetry_assets_v2 contains named descriptors, a 512x512 transparent RGBA symmetry_mask.png, a terrain-backed annotated symmetry_diagram.png, and categorical symmetry_regions.npz for every map. These are explanatory geometry assets, not battle-path or resource-influence labels, and are not added to selected training inputs. Source detections are unchanged. Ten presentation tests cover geometry and the gradient transform semantics; the full asset/seal/shape audit remains separate from scientific review.

Names distinguish single mirror, half-turn rotation, half-turn plus two mirror axes, fourfold rotation, and fourfold rotation plus four mirror axes. The last class partitions into eight fundamental wedges. A full square boundary admits at most fourfold rotation and four mirror axes; higher-order radial terrain patterns remain named and provisional instead of being clamped to that class. This catalog is not one ordinal quality scale; a mirror and a half-turn are different constraints of the same group size.

For a reflection that exchanges the selected bases, its axis is the perpendicular bisector of their joining segment. The diagram's colored axes are measured terrain axes. On mirror-only maps, a cyan dashed line shows the selected pair's bisector as a hypothesis; it does not rotate or move the measured axis. A/B are recorded scenario bases, with original source names retained in descriptor.json.

symmetry_catalog.csv lists every map. no_symmetry_match.csv lists all 118 unmatched cases with saved vault status, popularity, current membership and review reasons. All 118 have saved vault ranked=True; 94 are in the current curated release. These are significant review candidates, not proof of actual asymmetry. No threshold was loosened to manufacture a match. The broader review_required.csv has 209 maps, combining no-match, provisional geometry, tested-order ceiling and selected-pair mismatch reasons. Flags do not change curation or automatically exclude/approve training examples.

The source preview crop supplies the diagram's grayscale terrain context; region geometry comes from saved world-coordinate transforms. Diagrams are display assets. Numeric labels and original heights retain their own shapes.

EXTRACT-13 implications: proposed repair, not completed

Use the measured transform and valid domain rather than a universal half-map reflection. A rotational counterpart must be rotated, not reflected. Select the subgroup that preserves the actual selected 1v1 pair: a map's global symmetry may send that pair to other source slots. Existing one-direction correspondence checks do not certify scenario invariance. Multiple mates and roles still need EXTRACT-01 policy.

Path fragments must meet at shared, passable contact positions with continuous geometry and correct resource ownership. Transforming independently optimized fragments does not guarantee this. Therefore detected symmetry is a prerequisite for the EXTRACT-13 repair, not evidence the abrupt-end problem is already fixed.

Directional gradients — 26 October 2026

The owner's reference replaces flat sector fills with a bright-to-dark pattern whose direction follows each transform. A mirror repeats the same tangential gradient on both sides; a half-turn reverses its world-space direction on the opposite side. Higher-order rotation repeats a rotated seed. Reflection groups fold that seed into mirrored wedges and introduce cyan, amber and violet in addition to red. Axis-line accent colors identify individual measured axes.

The unannotated color field itself respects the detected group. Tests check mirror agreement, half-turn agreement with opposite gradient directions, fourfold rotation without an invented mirror, and two/four-axis reflection groups. Annotated axis colors and source-base labels are explanatory marks and are not themselves claimed to be a group-invariant field.

Each map additionally has symmetry_gradient.png: the pure RGB pattern without terrain or annotations. symmetry_mask.png is the pure transparent RGBA overlay; the annotated diagram composites it over terrain. Unsupported transform overlap has reduced overlay opacity. Unmatched maps receive no invented gradient. Old flat assets remain archived in terrain_symmetry_assets_v1. Labels, detected transforms, review flags and training profiles were not recalculated or changed.

Extracted source-layer registration - 26 October 2026

The owner accepted the symmetry feature for normal dataset inspection. It is no longer presented as an experimental feature. Select Symmetry under Compare layer across maps, or inspect it under Extracted source layers inside a map. The symmetry filter selects individual types, no-match maps or maps needing review. Existing owner notes/flags and curation are preserved.

Source-derived means calculated from the map's native terrain. It does not mean an explicit symmetry field exists in SCMAP, every detection is human-approved, or a selected model already consumes the descriptor. Recorded tolerances and individual unresolved cases remain visible. No new model training or extraction was run to perform this registration.
