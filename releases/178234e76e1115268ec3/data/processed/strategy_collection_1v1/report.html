Scope

Latest locally catalogued parent versions from the 8 October FAF snapshot are analysed; exact pool folders and version substitutions are recorded in the cohort manifest. Default setup is slot 1 versus its mirrored opponent, with only those two slots active and default adaptive options. Prefer 180-degree symmetry, tolerate small positional changes, then use an available reflection axis; an asymmetric map with exactly two slots uses its only opponent. Maps without a meaningful mirror are excluded. These are 1v1 structural estimates even where the original map is usually played with teams.

How to read the results

Affected mex share and distinct destinations are the main drop/air evidence. Sites are deduplicated across both attacking directions. Combined drops use the union of expansion and offensive opportunities; their shares must not be added. Water mexes are valid generic air-raid targets through torpedo aircraft. Representative gunship economics are used for the entire air-raid class, as requested.

The original absolute 1-5 scores remain in JSON/CSV. New relative 1-5 ratings are midrank percentiles of affected mex share in this analysed cohort, with zero always 1. This improves the interpretation of collection rankings but is not a calibrated universal measure of gameplay impact. A high share is an opportunity, not predicted damage or a win probability.

Front categories use the widest available representative route's sustained narrowest cross-section: closed below 24 map units, mixed 24-64, open at least 64. Disconnected land is separate. Counts refer to found route geometries, not fully independent fronts: routes may share sections, and symmetry can add paired geometry. The JSON retains signatures, merged sections, resource allocation and search limits. Widths are measured on representative paths, not optimized army-clearance routes.

Validation and limits

End-to-end validation checked 602 maps with 0 errors and 0 warnings. It checks two-slot/default resource scope, unique mex denominators, score arithmetic, fractional route-area conservation, registered terrain/overlay dimensions, and PNG/numeric-mask agreement. Four early numeric archives were reconstructed from approved cached paths and reproduced all scores exactly.

The pipeline retains .75 slope and amphibious depth 25, hover engineer water unloading, approximate map symmetry, merged route sections and 18 sensitivity cases. A streamed distance-field implementation preserves measurements while reducing memory. Symmetry signature collisions with a blocked centre retain valid mirrored polylines rather than inventing an illegal central route.

Limits: terrain-only movement does not simulate unit collisions, prop obstruction, steering, actual units/AA/intel, projectiles, economy upgrades or naval fire support. Known civilian defenses and special map restrictions are not simulated. Reclaim was not re-extracted and is explicitly null in this strategy export; it does not enter the impact score. Search-budget limits make route discovery incomplete. Special-purpose maps can have extreme geometric scores that do not describe ordinary competitive gameplay.

Artifacts include registered PNG overlays, raw opportunity and route JSON, numeric masks, metrics CSV, source hashes, selected configurations and explicit failure records. No replay scraping, new map-download attempts or hosted deployment was performed.
