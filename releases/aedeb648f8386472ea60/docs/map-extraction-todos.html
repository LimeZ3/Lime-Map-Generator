Accepted and presentation rules

  • Walk access and access structure are accepted by the user. Preserve their scientific meaning; the global mask slider changes display alpha only.
  • Global sidebar sliders range 0–100: background brightness defaults to 10 and mask opacity to 100. This supersedes the earlier fixed 50% fill rule. Layer PNGs have full base opacity; source tensor values and classifications stay unchanged.
  • Official vault context stays separate from tensor data; its brightness is independently adjustable (default 10%).
  • Each side-specific field must name its source slot. Normally blue is SLOT 1 and orange SLOT 2. Different or incorrect archived pairs must be explicit.
