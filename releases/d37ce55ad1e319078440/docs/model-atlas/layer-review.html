Section 1 review: existing evidence and pending changes

Historical review snapshot. The subsequent display correction is implemented; see current presentation methods for completed changes and remaining exact-version gaps. The default background preference was subsequently updated to a very dim, separately labeled FAF vault preview (10% opacity).

Reviewed 25 October 2026 against the five exact atlas versions. This is an inventory and TODO record. No extraction, scientific recomputation, tensor edits or PNG regeneration was performed. Access structure is a presentation rename; the saved gameplay field and existing anchors remain compatible.

Accepted in this review

Pathing and Surface appearance are accepted as currently presented. Retain the walk-access diagnostic with a clearer description; the explicit rename to Access structure has been applied without altering its stored arrays or PNGs.

Background rule

For starts, markers, props, wrecks and civilians, default to no map background (transparent layer over a neutral viewer surface). A dimmed official FAF vault preview may be offered as optional context; label the source and registration uncertainty. Do not use extracted height/terrain as an implicit background. This rule concerns entity/marker overlays; terrain, water, pathing and surface appearance still visualize their own numeric fields.

The current starts/markers/civilians/scenario PNGs were created by the new atlas renderer, not retrieved from an older preview library. The renderer explicitly applies terrain // 2 and draws points over it. The raw coordinates remain in the linked JSON records. Pending: replace those display backgrounds, without changing coordinates or extracted data.

Reclaim resolution and channels

The atlas renderer sums mass per 16×16 world-unit tile, producing only 32×32 cells for 10km maps and 64×64 for 20km maps before enlarging the PNG. This is an atlas presentation choice, not the resolution limit of the available data.

All five source caches contain native 512² or 1024² reclaim.npz arrays (known_mass_per_cell, known_energy_per_cell, entity counts and unknown-value counts), plus per-entity identities and values. The atlas diagnostics archive already retains native natural, tree_energy, rock_mass and wrecks arrays. The older transparent overlay library has 4-world-unit mass/energy tiles, rendered at native 512/1024 PNG dimensions.

  • TODO: expose separate natural energy and mass rows, using finer saved representations. Tree energy and natural-rock mass are also separate roles in the selected allocation model; document whether a source layer totals all natural prop mass or just the model's rock role.
  • TODO: keep wreck mass separate from natural mass and render it from its native role field. Generic library reclaim-mass PNGs can include multiple reclaim categories; do not relabel those as wreck-only or natural-only.
  • TODO: use no terrain background for entity overlays. Existing library PNGs are RGBA, but some legacy glyphs/legends and mixed entity semantics still need inspection before choosing one as the final atlas preview.

Air: restore three actual analyses

The displayed red/blue map is a new atlas distance proxy: (distance_to_B - distance_to_A) / map_diagonal. It is not the established air analysis and should be replaced in the atlas navigation.

For all five exact versions, the historical collection contains separate expansion drops, offensive drops and air raids, for each selected side. automatic_layers.npz retains medium_ARMY_*_expansion, medium_ARMY_*_offensive and medium_ARMY_*_air plus strict/permissive variants. auto_ARMY_*_{expansion,offensive,air}.png are existing overlays. opportunities.json records the setup, gates, response/economy assumptions and candidate/rejection reasons. DualGap's historical pair is ARMY_1 versus ARMY_12; other selected versions use ARMY_1 versus ARMY_2. These are not team-game labels.

The v1.2.1 annotation batch also has newer opportunity fields for Canis, Tabula and DualGap. Matching v1.2.1 jobs for Setons FAF and FAF Beetles were not found in the checked batch. Do not mix historical and newer calculations in an unlabeled comparison row.

Main battle paths

Saved strategy_collection_1v1/<key>/routes.json exists for all five versions, with land/amphibious/hover alternatives, widths and source starts. These are algorithmic route hypotheses, not observed army movement or a trained battle-path predictor. The historical DualGap selected pair has no land route; do not invent one or replace its scenario silently.

More recent own-half/frontier networks exist for Setons FAF, Canis, Tabula and DualGap in frontier_map_comparison/<key>/: result.json, K*_paths.png, K*_territories.png and K*_territory.npz. They encode contact-budget variants, shared approaches and response territories. A matching FAF Beetles case was not found in this comparison. Newer v1.2.1 fronts/fronts.json.gz exists for Canis, Tabula and DualGap; inspect status/disconnected cases before describing these as successful fitted fronts.

Main-base / rear / contestable resources

The actual whole-economy implementation contains per-side near_base, rear and forward_or_flank roles, resource identities, walking access and a one-engineer 300-second mass ledger. It is independent of the simple atlas land component. whole_economy/layers.npz stores ARMY_*_roles and ARMY_*_access; economy.json.gz retains resource and location records.

The current v1.2.1 batch has these fields for Canis, Tabula and DualGap. The seven-map starting-economy comparison also contains those three, but its Setons and Beetles entries are stock 433_560 and 4822_7169. They must not be shown as FAF 8553_17709 or 10838_21065. Matching whole-economy outputs for the two FAF versions were not found in the checked runs.

Historical opportunities.json also contains friendly/enemy/contested territory records. A forward/flank role, shared-neutral access and a contested opportunity region are different definitions; they cannot be silently merged into a single "contestable resources" target.
