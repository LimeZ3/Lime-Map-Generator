Parent popularity is not exact-version popularity

The vault exposes both map.gamesPlayed and mapVersion.gamesPlayed. We preserved both, without summing parent counts once per version. Among known family parents, the current parent is most played in 1,066 families and differs in 45. In the name-expanded search, the parent matches in 1,041 and differs in 70.

A recent improved release may have few exact-version plays while its parent design has years of usage. Raw version popularity can therefore favor an obsolete release. For the next freeze, a sensible primary preference is the most-played suitable design/parent, followed by its latest suitable version, retaining an older release only when it contributes useful distinct content. This is a selection recommendation, not an applied replacement rule. Existing human keep/exclude choices and field completeness remain relevant.

All counts are FAF's unfiltered all-time counters, across game configurations. They are not 1v1 quality scores, replay-filtered games or proof of competitive balance. Popularity alone must not override ranked/hidden/size checks, owner exclusions or missing training fields. The previous Canis RGB-availability issue remains a separate role-readiness finding.

Coverage and family-link evidence

The authoritative comparison pool is the 1,111 selected families and their kept members from the last deduplication audit. Their known parent IDs are definite links. A complete saved vault index from 8 October supplies additional candidates with matching normalized folder/display names; the owner-confirmed Canis alias includes 43 linked parents. We also queried all 35 parent records added beyond that inventory's highest ID and checked their names. Parents related by identity or these rules were refreshed live, with all referenced versions.

Outside-cohort name links are candidates, not a new content-validated family partition. Generic names, renamed older maps or unrelated similarly named maps can make expanded coverage imperfect. Prefer the within-kept-pool results for a direct comparison; inspect expanded winners before admitting them. A 256x256 hidden stock Canis winning the raw global counter does not qualify it for this square 512x512 release.

Capture is paced and non-transactional: counters may change during or after the 2.6-minute lookup. Parent counter totals are retained as published and are not silently replaced with sums of version counters. A version history was checked for complete relationship coverage; no failed requests were automatically retried.
