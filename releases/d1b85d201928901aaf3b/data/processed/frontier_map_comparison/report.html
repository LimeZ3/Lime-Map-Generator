Cross-map frontier-contact validation

Local default 1v1 tests, slot 1 versus the verified mirrored counterpart. This isolates the accepted Morning Prayer method: no reclaim, unit changes, new movement thresholds or tuned stopping threshold. Every contact budget 1–8 is retained. A budget is contacts per side, not necessarily complete connected attack routes.

Split at the perpendicular base bisector. Resource attraction uses only own-side eligible land mexes. Deployment terminates on the first real legal crossing; resource response comes from a selected contact or own base over the physical half-map graph. The equal terminal reward is never a physical teleport. Terrain territories have no fixed attention width. Exact symmetry is imposed on geometry and territories.

For rotation the mirrored contact is usually elsewhere along the frontier: the two half-approaches are displayed separately, without inventing a connection. Resources exactly on neutral frontier cells, or disconnected from the base within its own half, are listed separately. These scope changes mean cross-map absolute seconds are not a map quality ranking. All contacts assume an army is present; army splitting cost and deployment time are not charged.

Input limitations and exclusions

Sentina III and Adaptive Millennium remain omitted as requested. Badlands uses the requested 8 - Badlands_v4 (key 4_5), replacing the previously reviewed different map. Its substantial asymmetry is retained as an explicit scope exclusion, not repaired by altering terrain. Loki uses original v3, with scripted wall collision still omitted from the terrain grid. Forbidden Pass remains a land-only assessment; hover/amphibious fronts await a separate round. Near approaches within a few raster cells are recorded separately from actual vertex/diagonal intersections; shared-tree branches are not declared X crossings merely for passing nearby.
