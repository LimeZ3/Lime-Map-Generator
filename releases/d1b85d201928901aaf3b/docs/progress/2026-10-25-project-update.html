MapGen progress update — 25 October 2026

The MapGen project atlas is the main project reference for status, map layers, data, selected models, experimental branches, results and open work. The project remains experimental research software, not a finished or competitively validated generator.

This update records progress since the initial repository snapshot. Evidence is the atlas catalog and saved receipts, refreshed 2026-10-27T15:28:22+00:00.

Implemented and collected

First player-confirmed playable generated map - achieved 2026-10-25

A saved map produced by the staged learned-terrain, learned-marker and learned-reclaim-allocation pipeline loads and is judged playable by the player. First playable proof of concept, not competitive certification or evidence that every generated map is playable.

Evidence: docs/first-playable-map.md.

Map extraction and source representations

Terrain/water, starts, resource markers, natural energy/mass, wrecks, civilians and surface material representations have source receipts. Unknown values and unsupported runtime rules remain explicit.

Evidence: docs/map_layers_v2.md.

Four selected generation stages

Terrain/water, resource markers, natural reclaim allocation and wreck allocation have selected checkpoints. Deterministic finishing/composition exports geometry, markers and entities.

Evidence: docs/model-atlas/catalog.json.

Dataset and texture preparation

The selected curated cohort contains 1,020 training and 97 validation maps. Texture extraction completed 1,117 maps with no decode failures; 929 albedo sources are fully resolved. Extraction success does not certify every shader’s training semantics.

Evidence: docs/map_texture_tensors.md.

Gameplay-analysis and annotation pipeline

Versioned route/front, resource-economy and air/drop analyses exist. The 3,108-version annotation-pool milestone reports 2,764 scored maps, 329 exclusions and 15 failures. These are heuristic 1v1 observations, not map-quality ground truth.

Evidence: docs/map-rating-extraction/README.md.

Concluded all-in experiment: negative result

One 73.3M-parameter model jointly predicted 13 source channels from noise and 12 ratings, on 782 train / 69 validation maps. About 9.10 measured GPU-session hours including diagnostics did not produce usable maps. The detailed report preserves methods, compute, figures and limits. Research is closed; no checkpoint is promoted.

Evidence: docs/model-atlas/research/all-in/index.html.

Current evidence

  • 4 selected trained stages; 41 discovered checkpoint-bearing runs are preserved as an inventory, not 41 active pipeline stages.

  • 1020 train / 97 validation in the selected curated cohort. No independent final test set is being claimed.

  • 1117 texture extractions, 929 fully resolved albedo sources; learned painting is not selected.

  • 5 exact map versions across 19 source/derived comparison rows.

  • Separate joint experiment: 13 channels, 782 train / 69 validation; no selected production release.

  • MapGen Heatmap Medium 303: player assessment - "This map is actually playable. Not good, but also not terribly bad and IS playable." Frozen presentation export preserved; no claim of competitive certification.

  • Selected terrain geometry and natural reclaim composition have been observed in game; resource markers were exported and playtested, with balance still unproven.

  • Wreck allocation is trained and exported, but its current runtime behavior is not yet verified.

  • The joint one-map baseline reproduced a spatial template (height correlation 0.9980 at update 2,365), while sparse-density totals remained badly biased (Start A integral 139.6 instead of one). This is memorization evidence, not generalization.

  • The all-in research is closed with a negative full-generation result after 9.10 measured session hours and 667,632 map presentations. Lower LR avoided the old collapse through update 4,971, but fresh maps and quantity totals remained unusable. The complete report is in the atlas.

Next work

  • Preserve the first playable demonstration while further research and training-data improvements proceed; no new training is scheduled by the milestone.
  • Owner decisions: EXTRACT-07 symmetry is the first priority, including multiple reflection axes and radial sectors, model encoding and source-start correspondence. EXTRACT-05 slope is accepted with implementation pending. See docs/model-atlas/maps-data-gap-plan.md and docs/model-atlas/extraction-work.json. Extracted terrain-symmetry data now cover 1,310 audit maps; scientific review, start-pair policy and loader integration remain pending. No new training was started.
  • Solidify the current 10x10 km / 1v1 dataset as a named, versioned release with field readiness, non-square size classification and explicit validity; working name/ID are not a newly frozen dataset.
  • Inventory settings and declared/effective defaults for every dataset map; later configured resource-poor/rich examples may support static-map training. Generated adaptive maps remain out of scope.
  • Research a separate special-effects tensor with IDs/positions and supported parameters. Effect heatmap encoding is deferred.
  • Access rules, connected battle paths, economy-zone/contestability definitions and air scoring are deferred difficult work after symmetry. Team games are postponed; current scope is 1v1.
  • Specific atlas-map extraction reruns are out of scope. Additional augmentation is low priority; the terrain trainer already uses quarter-turn rotations/reflections, while pixel shifts require future validation.
  • Joint-source research is concluded for now; possible representation/objective changes are documented hypotheses, not scheduled training.
  • Learned texture painting, civilian composition and spawn-layout generation remain planned or experimental, with exact boundaries in the catalog.
  • Continue structuring completed work and the TODO list in this atlas; validate exports and gameplay rather than equating loss with playability.

The maps extraction work queue tracks known source/pairing/zoning issues and missing coverage. Reports using stock Setons/Beetles must not be presented as the selected FAF versions.
