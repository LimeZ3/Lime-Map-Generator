Obstacle-separated route prototype

Implemented 14 October 2026 after the drawn Archsimkats Valley example. Scope: that featured map, slots 1 to 2, land/amphibious/hover. No naval unit reach calculation or whole-library extraction is run by this tool.

Follow-up: the user's iterative corridor-cut algorithm and shared-chokepoint nodes are implemented in docs/corridor_cut_implementation.md. The new preview is in data/processed/navigation_corridors; this sampler stays as a comparison.

Definition

Alternative routes must pass on different sides of a substantial impassable terrain component. Merely displaced paths in the same open field are one family. Amphibious/hover passages through land-blocked areas are also retained as explicit new crossing choices. They are not claimed to be topologically distinct when the selected movement layer itself has no intervening barrier.

  1. Read cached raw passability and the two endpoint distance fields for each layer. Recover legal shortest-path predecessors in vectorized NumPy.
  2. Find eight-connected blocked components; choose an actual blocked cell as each barrier reference. Do not use a centroid that might sit in passable space.
  3. Sample a regular waypoint lattice and points beside barrier bounding boxes. Join cached paths start→waypoint→end and remove loops.
  4. Group candidates by signed ray-crossings around barrier references. Differences witness a blocked component between two paths. Open-ground variations merge. Keep the shortest sampled representative per signature, not a claim of the optimum over every path in that class.
  5. For non-land layers, additionally group by substantial land-blocked components crossed for at least six route cells. This exposes the two central water crossings. A component can contain both water and banks; this is a terrain passage annotation, not a water-body classification or a claim of navigability for every amphibious unit.
  6. Validate every resulting cell and adjacent step. Reject diagonal corner cuts. Export waypoints, lengths/detours, separating barrier IDs, new passage IDs and minimum/median centre clearance. SHA256 pins inputs and implementation.

Preview and results

data/processed/navigation_alternatives/index.html, served locally at http://127.0.0.1:5177/navigation_alternatives/index.html.

The default land examples show the 399.8-unit shortest route, two inner detours of 496.8/499.9 units, and two outer detours of 609.8/613.3 units. Amphibious and hover examples show the baseline and two new crossing candidates of 402.7/403.3 units. Both profiles happen to produce the same selected examples on this map. The viewer offers individual checkboxes, all candidates, barrier IDs and zoom.

The search found 13 land and 17 candidates each for amphibious/hover, from 486/531 sampled waypoints. These include branch combinations and local terrain detours; they are not 13 or 17 independent strategic attack corridors. Shared sections and chokepoints still need a corridor graph before producing training metrics.

Defaults: lattice spacing 24 tiles, maximum length 2.5× baseline; obstacle area at least 128 cells, or at least 16 cells spanning 32 tiles. Boundary-attached components cannot form a loop and are excluded from the barrier signature. These thresholds are parameters, not game balance rules. Smaller features are still blocked for paths but do not create separate report families.

The bounded sampler is not exhaustive: a pair of shortest-path trees and one waypoint can miss combinations of successive barrier choices. Ray counts also merge some complex multiply-winding classes. Do not convert the candidate count to a final route-diversity score. Later corridor-graph enumeration can recover missing combinations, consolidate branch choices and impose army-width criteria.

Navigation remains terrain-only, without props, unit footprints or engine steering. Clearance is distance from cell centres to blocked-cell centres, not validated corridor width or proof that a complete army can pass together.

Run and checks

.venv-codex/Scripts/python.exe -m supmapai.tools.inspect_route_alternatives
.venv-codex/Scripts/python.exe -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_strategic*.py' -v

Fifteen tests pass across navigation and route extraction. Route fixtures cover one family on an open field, two sides of a blocked island, distinct barrier signatures, water crossings relative to land, unreachable targets and illegal diagonal corners. All displayed Archsimkats paths also pass cell-by-cell validation.
