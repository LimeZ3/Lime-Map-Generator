Symmetric influence and merged route sections

Implemented for Thedion and Archsimkats Valley after the 14 October 2026 request. Existing raw routes are preserved in routes_before_normalization.json in each output folder. Reruns start from that snapshot, avoiding cumulative deformation.

Symmetry normalization

Detect involutive transformations that exchange the selected spawns: 180-degree rotation, horizontal/vertical reflection, and diagonal reflections for square maps. Accept at most 5% passability mismatch, blocked-region IoU at least 0.65, and a spawn displacement at most two map units. Highly asymmetric layouts are not forced into symmetry simply because most of the map is open terrain.

The influence grid is the intersection of the raw grid and its transformation: a cell remains blocked if either corresponding source cell blocks it. Source terrain is retained unchanged. This is an explicitly normalized influence model, not a claim that the game uses the same mask.

Rerun corridor discovery on that grid. Close the route families under symmetry. Paired routes use precisely transformed polylines; a self-symmetric family uses a half-path to a fixed-axis cell or central edge and its reflected half. Thedion requires a one-unit adjustment of the second route endpoint to exchange the spawns exactly. Actual resource/spawn markers are retained at stored positions.

Territory membership is closed under the same transformation and route-ID permutation. Corresponding routes have exactly equal allocated areas. Allocation uses integer membership-count histograms so mirrored summation order cannot introduce a numerical area difference.

Merged sections

For each route pair, locate monotone aligned runs no farther than eight units apart. The connecting segment must remain on passable terrain: close paths on opposite sides of a cliff are not merged. Require at least 12 units of run length. Use a canonical existing centerline and short valid connectors to snap the other route onto it. Remove loops, enforce symmetry again and validate final geometry. Two passes handle interactions between snapping and symmetric counterparts.

Extract maximal exact shared-edge chains with stable IDs M1, M2, etc. Each section contains its polyline, length and participating route IDs. Each route records merged_section_ids and ordered merged_section_uses, including point-index intervals and direction. The final graph is rebuilt at shared-section endpoints, preserving a prior chokepoint's representative node when it moves within eight units. Route edge sequences reconstruct the complete final polylines exactly.

The viewer draws each merged section once in pale gray, labels it and explicitly lists which sections each route uses. A checkbox toggles these labels/centerlines. Area sharing and physical section merging remain different concepts.

Results

Thedion retains three route families. Routes 1 and 2 are exact counterparts: both 627.0925 units long with 82,435.8333 allocated square units. Route 3 is self-symmetric, 695.0509 units long. M1 is used by routes 1/3; M2 by routes 2/3. Both merged sections are 79 units long.

Archsimkats retains five land and seven amphibious/hover families. The paired outer routes now have identical mirrored geometry and exactly equal areas. There are six merged land sections and fourteen for each non-land layer.

Locations and counts of actual mexes/hydros are not artificially symmetrized. Sites outside the normalized reachable grid remain explicitly unassigned. This avoids inventing resources to make a statistic symmetric.

Validation and outputs

All 35 targeted route/navigation/metric tests pass. New tests cover approximate symmetry detection, rejection of asymmetric features, snapping nearby centerlines, no merging through walls, unchanged raw masks, exact symmetric geometry/areas, and preservation of chokepoints and their branch combinations after normalization. Both actual maps were additionally checked for exact partner geometry and area equality, membership-bitset symmetry and full graph-route reconstruction.

normalized_masks.npz contains the normalized analysis grids. territories.npz contains their route memberships. routes.json records symmetry detection, normalization rules, original/normalized endpoints and the merged section graph. PNG overlays and width/resource statistics are recomputed from final geometry.
