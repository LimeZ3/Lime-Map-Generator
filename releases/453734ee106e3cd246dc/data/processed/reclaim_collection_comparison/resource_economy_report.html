Five-minute economic pull: construction and reclaim

Fixed1s acquisition / three tiers; one existing UEF T1 engineer shared across the eligible own-side pool for300s. Reference mex:36 mass,360 energy,12 engineer-s to build,2 mass/s income and2 energy/s upkeep. Values come from the pinned FAF source listed in each result.

One mex-sized mass advance starts the schedule and is excluded from economic gain. Construction mass must be affordable before building; existing mex production or completed reclaim can fund the next mex. A greedy feasible schedule compares incremental horizon return per occupied engineer-second. It is not an optimum or an actual match-income prediction.

Energy costs/upkeep and known companion reclaim energy are ledgered. Power is assumed supplied; no energy stalls, mass-to-energy conversion, engineer production cost, travel, upgrades, enemy denial or overflow are simulated. The existing mass/25+1 reclaim work hypothesis remains. Tied identical tasks receive expected fractional route attribution, without parallel engineers or arbitrary asymmetric ordering.
