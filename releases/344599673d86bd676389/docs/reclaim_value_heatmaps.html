Learned Reclaim Value Heatmaps

Current Contract

The neural network learns where, the future composer decides what and how much. Begin with two joint output roles:

  • Tree reclaim energy allocation, weighted by extracted energy values.
  • Natural-rock reclaim mass allocation, weighted by extracted mass values.

These are not entity-count PDFs. A large energy-bearing tree group or rich rock contributes proportionally to its physical value. Wreck mass and living civilian structures are later roles, not folded into the natural-rock target.

No blueprint-ID, orientation or instance-pose learning is needed for this MVP. The earlier asset-learning proposal in entity_asset_learning_plan.md is now an alternative, not the active plan.

Existing Data, No Raw-Map Rerun

Source: data/tensors/reclaim_props_curated_256_v1. Use unclipped physical channels 2 (trees_energy) and 4 (rocks_mass), never the old clipped log targets. Both channels have fully known values in the curated cohort.

Sum-pool 256 to 128, then divide each nonempty role by its per-map total. A field therefore describes the share of the map's tree energy or rock mass allocated to each cell. Multiplying by any chosen budget restores a physical target grid. The sums, unknown-value masks, file hashes and family splits are checked during preparation. Totals are retained for audit, not fed to the network.

The frozen 1,020 training / 97 validation map split remains unchanged. Human exclusions and preferred-version deduplication are inherited. Raw maps and prior models are untouched. A 128 grid represents four world units per cell on these 512-world-unit (10km) maps. Original small source raster blur is retained; there is no clipping, new broad smoothing or inferred asset painting.

First Model

supmapai.ml.reclaim_heatmaps is a 4.72M-parameter, two-output multiscale U-Net. Inputs: encoded height, water distance/depth, land/hover passability, the two planned spawn fields, and mex/hydro fields. No source reclaim target is an input.

The bounded first pilot runs for 300 seconds after loading. It minimizes spatial cross entropy, Hellinger distance and sum-pooled allocation cross entropy at multiple scales. Roles are balanced; empty source categories have no spatial loss. A user-selected zero budget will produce no objects; this network does not predict category absence or total quantities.

This is a deterministic conditional allocation baseline, not diffusion and not an autoencoder fed its own target. It can learn terrain-dependent spatial preferences, but ambiguous training layouts can average into broad fields. It does not yet produce different neural heatmaps for the same terrain/seed. Do not hide that failure mode by sharpening, filtering or placing props before reviewing the raw outputs. If coherence remains weak, a stochastic, cluster-scale heatmap model is the next architectural experiment, not asset-ID supervision.

Evaluation

Review six reproducibly sampled held-out maps beside their extracted targets, plus the approved generated seed-303 terrain conditioned on learned mex/hydro positions. The generated example has no ground-truth reclaim layout. Show raw predictions and the existing 180-degree average symmetry projection separately.

All PNG panels use one fixed logarithmic color scale, zero to 32 times uniform allocation. White saturates above that scale; the saved float arrays do not. No independent panel contrast stretching. No terrain mask, placement validator, asset sampling, quantity matching or shoreline rejection is applied.

Full validation measurements include spatial NLL, Hellinger distance, source allocation captured by the hottest predicted 10% of area, and predicted allocation within nonzero source support. Also compare wrong-map conditioning and a train-only mean-map baseline. Beating uniform alone is not enough. These are allocation metrics, not predicted entity counts or gameplay balance.

Larger 30-Minute Experiment

User review accepts center attraction and terrain-feature attachment as useful, not artifacts to automatically remove. Trees may be evenly spread or patchy. Sparse source placement is not necessarily the desired aesthetic result, and reconstruction metrics measure source agreement, not ultimate gameplay quality. No map is removed or downweighted based on this feedback in this run. Underwater reclaim remains an unconstrained prediction; the model may inherit the source dataset's scarcity of underwater natural reclaim.

The frozen targets and all nine condition fields remain unchanged. Train a fresh base-64 U-Net (17,857,858 parameters, 3.78 times the pilot's capacity) for 1,800 seconds with batch 8, BF16 and the same objective/augmentation. A synthetic GPU preflight passed; the live run's observed peak allocation was about 2.3 GB, within the existing 5 GB guard. Retain per-minute validation, best and final checkpoints. The best checkpoint may precede the final update; show both if their behavior diverges. More time does not guarantee better unseen-map results.

Human Selection: Latest Checkpoint Preferred

On 24 October 2026 the user preferred the paused update-20,657 predictions: sharper heatmaps attach reclaim to hills, cliffs and mountains and suppress allocation over flat plains. The small pilot's broad rock allocation across the battlefield is less desirable. For the MVP, this terrain selectivity is more useful than closely reproducing every source map's particular placement.
