Anchor-assisted symmetry v2 - 26 October 2026

This implements the owner's correction: rated maps can genuinely be asymmetric, including resources. Rating/ranked status is not an input. A layout is never assigned a symmetry merely because it is rated or because an earlier terrain label exists. Terrain, source layout and selected default configuration have separate evidence and conditions.

Fixed method and evidence

  1. Reuse the sealed earlier terrain scan and verify its contract/output hashes. Verify the native NPZ, source metadata and marker identity. Propose whole-square groups, earlier fitted groups and mirror bisectors suggested by source starts. Test whole generated groups, not one isolated matching transform.
  2. Match starts, mexes and hydros separately, using one-to-one correspondence within 4 world units. There is no unmatched-marker percentage allowance. Bijective nearest matching is used where possible; a sparse minimum-cost matching resolves collisions. Missing hydros contribute no evidence. Approximate layout confirmation requires at least two starts, four mexes, nontrivial point movement and anchor span of at least 20% of the map extent.
  3. Separate full source starts/resource pools from the recorded selected starts and active resource configuration. A fourfold source map can have a smaller 1v1 configuration group. Default confirmation tests the entire selected pair, not one-direction matching from A to B. Whether a default transform actually exchanges the two bases is recorded independently.
  4. Require at least 99.5% continuous playable-domain preservation. Approximate interior radial patterns remain separate terrain evidence rather than silently discarding square corners. The current audit cohort is full-square 10km maps; rectangular and arbitrary cropped-domain support remains a separate task.
  5. Keep the original height fit. The old terrain-close limits remain 0.055 weighted normalized RMSE and 0.12 normalized p95. The layout fallback also has broad terrain guards: RMSE <=0.20 and p95 <=0.40, normalized by robust relief. These are bounded screening policies, not calibrated confidence probabilities.
  6. Check cached native land/hover masks and water occupancy. Land/hover mismatch must be <=8% of the map and <=15% of the passable union. Disagreement farther than two native cells from a source boundary must cover <=1% of the map. Water mismatch must cover <=2% of the map; when the wet union covers >=1%, its relative mismatch must also be <=15%.
  7. Compare endpoint connectivity partitions on native land/hover masks at clearances 0, 1, 2 and 4 cells, using four-neighbor connectivity. The un-eroded check allows a recorded endpoint snap of at most two cells. Wider clearance checks use endpoints passable on both sides. These checks can reject a marker-symmetric map whose routes disconnect or whose wide connection changes.
  8. Check spawn-to-marker travel costs on a conservative four-cell grid. A change greater than 8 world units + 20% of the shorter cost is suspected material asymmetry. Coarse-grid conflicts are always recomputed at native resolution before rejection; coarse rasterization alone must not close an opening and manufacture a veto. Only finite comparable costs are used for this cost test; native connectivity checks separately handle reachability.

The thresholds are a frozen first policy for this version, with code/settings and source pins saved in its contract. They were not selected from map ratings or changed to reduce the remaining no-match count. Largest complete groups are tested first, canonical proposals before pixel-fitted alternatives. Smaller alternatives are not re-evaluated after both scopes have a larger verified group. Untested dominated candidates are recorded as such, not as failed matches.

Data and model roles

Every result preserves the previous terrain_descriptor, the new source_layout and default_layout, selected scope, match quality, anchor inventory, movement evidence, group rejection reasons and original source-slot names. No height is averaged or mirrored, no marker is moved and no opponent is replaced.

Per-map NPZs retain the 28-value condition plus separate 28-value terrain_condition and default_condition. The aggregate conditions.npz contains the same arrays in pinned map-key order, with availability. The primary condition describes the supported full-source group, or selected-default group if only that scope is supported. Terrain-only and unresolved primary conditions are unavailable/zero-filled. The terrain vector preserves the earlier detector's semantics, including its legacy bounded no-match label; it is not newly certified as a gameplay condition. Future loaders must choose the appropriate scope.

The selected models still use their actual existing inputs. This change does not add the vectors to a checkpoint, start training, drop maps, modify existing loss masks, or replace the original terrain/resource training targets. A symmetric layout is allowed to retain artistic height/texture/prop asymmetry.

Dataset review

The extracted Symmetry PNG layer stays in the atlas source-layer selector. New filters show approximate layouts, terrain-only results and recovered height-only no-matches. Detail views show all three scopes, not a single forced symmetry label. Approximate, unresolved, terrain-only and configuration-conflict cases retain explicit review reasons; no owner notes or flags are cleared.

Current PNG release: data/processed/terrain_symmetry_assets_v3. It keeps the directional-gradient convention. Catalogs include symmetry_catalog.csv, no_symmetry_match.csv, no_supported_layout.csv and changes_from_terrain_baseline.csv. Earlier scans and PNG releases remain saved.

Limits and tests

This checks the recorded static source/configuration, cached land/hover movement, water occupancy and selected clearances. It does not certify engine navigation, combat balance, naval depth-specific movement, every amphibious profile, scripted walls/effects, reclaim symmetry, or every adaptive setting. A finite travel-cost sample and high mask overlap cannot prove complete tactical equivalence. Existing source/script uncertainty and dataset review issues remain visible.

Seven new tests cover approximate-layout acceptance, a smaller default subgroup, genuinely asymmetric resources even when marked ranked, preservation of no-match, one-to-one matching, a single mirror that must not become a half-turn, a moved chokepoint with unchanged endpoint connectivity but changed travel cost, and flat terrain without anchors. The earlier terrain tests still pass. Integrity checks verify all sealed outputs, frozen source identities, affine transforms and vectors.

The parent cohort inherited a previous half-map compatibility filter. These counts are not an unbiased estimate of symmetry prevalence across the FAF vault.

Implementation: layout detector, collector, tests. Earlier Cinder measurements remain the motivating case study; its current match is now approximate rather than a height-only no-match.
