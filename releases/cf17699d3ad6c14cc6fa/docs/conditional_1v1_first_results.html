First Conditional 1v1 Results

Date: 23 October 2026. The first training/data milestone is implemented and tested. The complete proposed roadmap is not finished: generated gameplay scoring, reliable controls, improved terrain and playable showcase exports remain ahead. Cross-machine seed matching is deferred at the user's request.

Data

The frozen strategy release produced 2,077 full-playable-area 10km versions across 1,931 detected families: 1,688 train, 170 validation and 219 test. Another 159 512-square maps have cropped playable arenas and were excluded; 528 scored maps have other dimensions. All packed maps passed conservation, native alignment, family, train-only normalization, archive integrity and consumed source-stage hash checks. Initial reclaim is complete on 1,882 versions; the reclaim model trains on 1,541 training versions and evaluates on 147 validation versions.

These are supported two-player configurations, not a human-curated collection of competitive 1v1 designs. Source maps may support more players. No label is an overall quality score. The test split was packed and audited but not fitted, tuned or used in the model comparisons.

Dataset: data/tensors/strategy_10km_1v1_v1/. Audit: audit.md, audit.json. Source previews: source_contact_sheet.png.

Training Experiments

The first four-model experiment used epsilon prediction for 1,000 updates each. It reduced validation loss but collapsed to approximately 98.6% water during fresh-noise generation. Increasing sampling to 200 steps did not fix it.

The second experiment uses velocity prediction, bottleneck attention and 2,000 updates per model: profile-free terrain, conditioned terrain, resources and reclaim. GPU training took about 505 seconds in total on the RTX 5080; peak allocated GPU memory was under 600 MiB. All checkpoints/configs/progress and model/data hashes are retained. Training support is still small and these are early experiments, not final models.

Latest artifacts: data/models/strategy_10km_1v1_v2_smoke/. The directory contains experiment_report.md, model checkpoints, full-validation profile-ablation results and matched-profile galleries. The main two sweeps each use four independent seeds with three settings, giving 24 conditional outputs. Another six outputs provide a profile-free terrain comparison.

What Worked

The latest mex-profile gallery averages 25.1% water and 37.2% structurally land-passable terrain. Sources are not encoded into the samples: all start from fresh noise. Geometry, exact source configurations, sparse resources, reclaim stock and annotations now connect through the same training/generation loop.

Increasing reclaim requested through the profile increased generated handling-adjusted reclaim at every low/medium/high step for all four test seeds. No requested reclaim budget is enforced by the decoder; it only caps adjusted stock at raw mass. Mean per-player results are:

Setting Requested Adjusted Stock Generated Adjusted Stock
Low 1,813 6,184
Medium 5,025 8,619
High 10,698 12,293

This is preliminary directional steering over four seeds, with substantial calibration error. It does not establish human gameplay relevance or generalize to every requested profile combination.

Full-validation profile ablation found modest benefits for terrain and reclaim. Resource placement performed similarly with correct versus shuffled labels. Exact mex counts of 24/36/50 per player in the gallery are decoder instructions, not evidence of learned count control.

Remaining Problems And Next Gate

Shorelines are fragmented/noisy, some spawns need large repairs, and several generated layouts have no structural land connection between spawns despite the requested land-focused profile. The raw resource-density integral is badly calibrated. Reclaim is directionally controllable but overshoots and remains a density field rather than realized blueprint entities. Full game collision, resource fairness, actual battle fronts and air opportunity have not been validated on these outputs. No maps from this experiment were installed into FAF.

The next work should improve the physical terrain representation and shoreline consistency, strengthen spawn/connectivity supervision, and use a sparse/count-aware resource objective. Then measure the full frozen gameplay teacher on assembled outputs and calibrate the controls. Longer training and 256x256 refinement follow once fresh-noise terrain and repair burden improve. Playable showcase export is gated on those results; it is not justified by denoising loss alone.
