Map texture tensors v1

The extractor reads painted terrain materials directly from SCMAP files. It does not infer texture labels from lit preview pictures or geometry. It supports the structural layouts of versions 52, 53, 56 and 60 and stops after the paint images, so damaged later props or skyboxes do not affect texture decoding.

The first five-map smoke covered Adaptive Canis River, Adaptive Twin Rivers, Adaptive Tabula Rasa, Adaptive Seton's Clutch and Adaptive Pandemonium Pass. All five decoded, passed NPZ read-back checks, and resolved their referenced albedo assets from the local game installation. The initial smoke used 128; the current review and production format use 256×256, aligned with the newer full-map terrain/resource pipeline.

Production cohort: data/tensors/terrain_curated_20261024_v1, 1,117 maps with 1,020 training / 97 validation examples. Preserve its corrected curated families and splits, including reassigned families. Original map provenance is joined by key from the historical strategy dataset; it must match the original family/split fields, rather than undoing the later curation. No test manifest is read and no model training is started.

Output: data/tensors/map_textures_curated_256_v2. The partial stock-only v1 pass was paused after 211 maps and is superseded; it is retained for provenance, not a training release. V2 mounts the locally installed stable FAF texture packs as overrides of the stock archives.

The five-map 256 pilot passed the same checks as the initial smoke, and the lazy published-dataset loader was exercised with a rotated, reflected half crop on all five maps. Texture-specific tests cover all four binary versions, DDS channel order, threshold-before-pooling, layer occlusion, missing/custom assets, unsafe paths, augmentation alignment, sealed output reuse and corruption detection.

Tensor contract

Each map has maps/<key>/textures.npz, loadable with allow_pickle=False. Channel-first spatial arrays use [channel,z,x], covering the full map with no flip or transpose. Tensors are in [0,1], not [-1,1]. NPZ string arrays carry paths without Python pickles. No material IDs are encoded as interpolated floats.

Array Shape Meaning
mask_bytes_native 8×native-H×native-W, uint8 Exact decoded DDS RGBA channels; absent legacy second mask is zero padded
mask_samples 8×256×256 Area-averaged raw samples / 255, useful for reversible paint targets
blend_alpha 8×256×256 Standard-shader decoded alpha, pooled after the nonlinear transform
paint_weights 9×256×256 Lower material plus eight strata, after sequential occlusion; before upper material alpha
paint_weights_valid scalar bool Whether the shader's paint semantics are implemented
active_layers 9 bools Shader-active paint slots; legacy TTerrain uses only four overlays
palette_albedo_rgba 10×4×64×64 Local material swatches, including alpha; zeros require checking availability
palette_asset_available 10 bools Whether each albedo asset decoded successfully
palette_tiling_valid 10 bools Resolved albedo with valid positive tiling scale
palette_albedo_paths, palette_normal_paths 10 strings each Material identity in normalized virtual paths
palette_albedo_scale, palette_normal_scale 10 floats each Original SCMAP tiling scales
albedo_rgb 3×256×256 Diagnostic unlit tiled material appearance
albedo_validity 1×256×256 Fraction of contributing material color known; missing upper asset conservatively invalidates RGB
upper_alpha 1×256×256 Sampled alpha of resolved upper material

Modern palette slots are lower, strata 0..7, upper. Legacy six-slot palettes are mapped to lower, strata 0..3, upper (slots 0..4 and 9), preserving original paths and scales. Unused slots are explicit. Slot 1 is not a universal grass/rock/snow class: models must receive the palette or another deliberate material embedding. Swatches and scale vectors are a first palette-conditioning representation, not an implemented texture generator.

Shader semantics

The standard TTerrain and TTerrainXP albedo code transforms mask samples by clamp(2*sample-1,0,1) and blends later painted layers over earlier layers. This is verified in the FAF terrain shader. The derived weights therefore form a partition of one, but the raw mask channels do not. Compute the nonlinear transform and occlusion at native mask resolution, then pool; reversing those operations would lose thin painted features.

The upper material has its own tiled texture alpha and is deliberately separate from the nine paint proportions. Normal-map references are retained; normal-map image tensors and normal blending are not implemented. Advanced/custom shaders may repurpose texture slots for roughness or terrain information. Their raw masks and palettes are still extracted, but derived weight validity is false. Their zero derived weights are not targets describing an empty/unpainted map.

Assets, previews and limitations

Stock albedos are resolved case-insensitively from local env.scd/textures.scd. Current-map custom /maps/<folder>/... assets are supported with bounded safe path resolution. Installed stable FAF env.nx2 and textures.nx2 are mounted after stock archives, with their paths and hashes pinned in the contract. --asset-archive supplies explicit ordered overrides; --stock-only disables automatic FAF packs. No downloads occur. Asset source, virtual path, image size and SHA-256 are recorded. Missing assets and decode failures are explicit, and do not prevent preserving the map's paint masks. A missing asset must not become an invented material, and beta/development pack variants are not guessed.

vault_preview.png is the map's embedded preview; it can be an author-provided image and is not necessarily the latest server thumbnail. albedo.png is an unlit diagnostic using bilinear tiled sampling, not a game render or a new RGB training target with validated appearance equivalence. It omits decals, water surface, props, lighting, shadows, normal shading and texture mipmap filtering. Tiny tiling patterns can alias at the output resolution. paint_weights.png shows slot-weight colors; palette.png shows the referenced material swatches.

Biome classes are not inferred from palette order or path names. Mixed palettes are legitimate. Decals can make a large visual contribution and are counted as omitted in metadata. They require a separate future layer. The terrain shader's upper alpha/specularity and advanced rendering channels must not be confused with surface material coverage.

Full-map texture painting may be asymmetric even when terrain is symmetric. augment_arrays applies exactly the same rotation/flip and optional half crop as the geometry, while leaving material palette order unchanged. Never impose symmetry on an existing texture target as a side effect of reading it. Native masks are omitted from augmented batches rather than left at a conflicting pose.

Running and resuming

$env:PYTHONPATH='src'
.venv-codex/Scripts/python.exe -m supmapai.tools.extract_map_textures --out data/tensors/map_textures_curated_256_v2

The same command resumes sealed map outputs without recomputing them. Input, game archive and code hashes must match the contract; changed sealed files are rejected. A controller OS lease prevents duplicate runs. Per-map failures are recorded and skipped on resume, never retried automatically. To pause, create pause.request in the output directory; remove it before explicit resume. Status, manifests and a local HTML index update every 25 maps. Decode audit and semantic coverage are separate: successfully decoding an advanced shader does not certify the derived weights. The complete manifests preserve curated family splits.

For a persistent hidden run and readable status, use:

.\scripts\map-textures.ps1 -Action Resume
.\scripts\map-textures.ps1 -Action Status
.\scripts\map-textures.ps1 -Action Pause

Load a published map with:

from supmapai.tensors.map_textures import TextureDataset
dataset = TextureDataset("data/tensors/map_textures_curated_256_v2", "train")
arrays, row = dataset.get(0)  # also accepts rotation, flip, half
# Model targets require arrays["paint_weights_valid"] to be true.
# row["terrain_archive"] and its hash pair the frozen terrain with this paint.

The release is published only after the entire requested cohort decodes without failures. Material/shader validity flags must still be honored on that release. The extractor does not train a model or modify geometry/resource datasets.
