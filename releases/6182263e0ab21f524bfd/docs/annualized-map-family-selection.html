Final map-family selection by plays per year

Owner decision applied on 27 October 2026. 1,189 maps retained across 1,111 families, with no more than two examples per family. There are 65 new exclusions, plus 56 preserved owner exclusions: 121 excluded in total. The shared atlas selection was updated through its authenticated owner controls and independently read back. All 1,310 decisions match; manual symmetry suggestions were preserved unchanged.

Selection rule

For each exact map version:

plays_per_year = mapVersion.gamesPlayed / ((as_of - mapVersion.createTime) / 365.2425 days)

The common as-of timestamp is 2026-10-27 16:41:33.939668 UTC, the end of the captured live FAF lookup. Use the version upload/release time, not last update time or its parent's date. Parent-map play totals are not version counters. Every kept candidate has a valid release date and exact-version counter.

Within each existing audited family, retain the highest annualized rate. Retain at most one additional example: the highest-rate alternative with a verified materialized terrain/water, paint, default resource/start, or reclaim difference from the leader. This preserves the owner's earlier variety requirement for second versions. Different inactive adaptive option inventories alone do not justify a second example. Existing duplicate-audit comparison tolerances are unchanged. Ties use lifetime exact plays, then stable map key. There is no newness, adaptive, FAF branding or texture-completeness preference.

Candidates are the 1,254 owner-kept members of the existing 1,310-map audit cohort. This does not add more vault history or restore any of the 56 owner exclusions. Existing family splits are preserved: 1,083 train candidates and 106 validation candidates. Seventy-eight families retain a second version; 41 primary representatives change from the old curated selection.

Interpretation and remaining work

Plays/year is the owner's final representative-selection criterion. It measures average observed lifetime usage, not recent growth, intrinsic quality or a causal player preference: availability, listing visibility and years of game activity can affect it. FAF upload time approximates release, and counters need not cover exactly the same historical observation window. No age floor or statistical shrinkage is applied. These caveats do not change the chosen ranking rule.

No source map was deleted. Existing checkpoints, labels and tensor releases remain immutable and may refer to the previous representatives. Next, audit per-field readiness and rebuild the named dataset's role manifests/tensors from this selection, including effective human symmetry/start overrides and slope. Missing RGB or other role data must be explicit; it must not silently substitute a lower-popularity version. No model training was started by this operation.
