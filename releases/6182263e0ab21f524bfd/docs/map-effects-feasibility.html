Extract-12: bounded effects feasibility probe

27 October 2026. Dataset map selection (Extract-06b) is owner-finalized and closed. This probe uses those 1,189 selected versions. It executes no map Lua, downloads no maps and does not change training inputs.

No structural wave/marker inventory failures across 1,189 maps. The candidate union overlaps categories. Absence of these signatures is not proof of no effects: effects can come from imported game scripts, unit/prop classes, custom blueprint scripts or generated runtime behavior outside this bounded scan.

Storage and mapping

  1. Save markers: _save.lua → Scenario.MasterChain...Markers. Effect markers can carry EffectTemplate, position, offset, orientation, and scale. Weather Definition markers describe weather names/chances/drift; Weather Generator markers supply origins and cloud parameters. A weather definition's position is not an effect footprint.
  2. Placed effect props: position comes from the SCMAP prop instance. An associated *_script.lua may call CreateEmitterAtBone with an emitter .bp path. Retain both the placed prop ID and the emitter ID; they are different things. Some emitters are placed directly as emitter blueprints. Existing extraction already identifies six such instances in Adaptive High Hovis.
  3. Arbitrary map Lua: emitter calls can attach to props/entities, depend on options, move, recur over time, or use randomized target objects. Merely finding the code does not establish default activation or fixed coordinates.
  4. Shoreline waves: .scmap binary wave-generator section before terrain layers. Position, rotation, velocity, texture/ramp strings and ten remaining float parameters are recoverable. This probe preserves those floats without assigning unverified parameter semantics. These are separate from the binary water settings, terrain textures, decals and skybox.

Source evidence is the local selected map files, the existing structural SCMAP reader, and saved entity extraction. See also the game's emitter utility usage for examples of attached emitters, blueprint paths, scale and lifetime changes. No general emitter behavior or visual radius is inferred from its filename.

Easy subset: locations can be heatmapped

Recovered custom prop-script anchor locations:

  • Adaptive Esais Vengeance v13: 9 rain anchors.
  • Adaptive High Hovis v1: 37 rain/vapor anchors (the six direct emitter records above are included, not additional).
  • Cityscape Duel v7: 9 rain anchors.

The prototypes save 1 × 256 × 256 anchor heatmaps at native x/z positions, with a fixed three-pixel Gaussian for illustration only. Orange dots mark origins over terrain. This is not rendered effect coverage, intensity, physical size, time persistence, or proof that an effect is active. No rotation/canonicalization or symmetry replication was applied. IDs and raw positions remain in JSON.

Stop rule and recommendation

The simple placed-origin subset is feasible. The general layer is not a single straightforward mask. For example, Adaptive Twilight Hill attaches probabilistic, temporary lightning emitters to surviving crystals. Several adaptive scripts share volcano functions with option-dependent behavior. Weather moves and drifts; an origin heatmap cannot represent its full visible extent. Even a custom prop's script being present does not prove an instance is active under engine defaults.

Defer the general effects tensor at this low priority. Keep this inventory and the three prototypes. Do not add empty effect targets to the remaining maps or claim absent effects as supervised negatives. A future implementation can begin with explicit static anchors and validity masks; defaults/runtime interpretation belongs with Extract-14/11 rather than this quick probe. No effects model is trained and no source-layer training contract is changed.
