Joint Height And Water Training

The recorded checkpoint uses legacy resource-dependent conditioning. New terrain-only work should use the direct terrain target schema and derive labels from the actual encoded target representation. This document remains a record of the existing experiment, not a claim it has been retrained.

This terrain-only experiment keeps height, water boundary and depth in one diffusion network. Spawn/resource/reclaim/texture models do not train in this run. The existing annotated 10km cohort and family splits remain unchanged.

Representation

The source is data/tensors/strategy_10km_1v1_v1, with 1,688 training versions, 170 validation versions and 219 test versions. The prepared terrain dataset is data/tensors/terrain_joint_10km_1v1. Packing verifies source archive checksums. It stores these three simultaneous outputs at 128x128:

  • Signed physical height relative to the source sea plane, encoded with asinh. The 4-world-unit scale preserves ordinary relief while accommodating tall cliffs.
  • Signed shoreline distance in world units, positive inside water and negative on land, capped at 64 units. This is the learned water layer. Its sign supplies a water mask; the smooth distance field supplies shoreline context.
  • Positive water depth with reversible log scaling around 2 world units.

Height/depth limits and conditioning statistics use the training split only. No per-map height stretching is introduced. The original physical/native tensors remain intact. Re-encoding does not alter the frozen automated rating policy.

The two requested base locations are spatial conditions, represented by broader Gaussian fields. They are not spawn predictions. Gameplay labels remain optional profile conditions with their presence masks. Direct structural controls for water fraction and height relief are added from the training tensors; these are not new human quality ratings. The sample sweep requests 0%, 20% and 40% water at typical training-cohort relief with the same other conditions and starting noise.

Model And Compute

The larger normalized residual U-Net has 2,986,739 parameters, base width 48, embedding width 256, and global bottleneck attention. The run uses velocity prediction, a cosine noise schedule, bfloat16 mixed precision on the RTX 5080, AdamW, 500-update warm-up, learning-rate decay, gradient clipping and EMA weights. Cross-machine seed identity is out of scope. Local matched-noise samples remain useful for comparing model behavior.

The main objective is joint denoising. Supervised gradient reconstruction for height/water emphasizes coherent source features rather than indiscriminate smoothing. A small sign-consistency term discourages height and water from disagreeing away from shore. Base neighborhoods receive extra reconstruction weight. These are structural training hypotheses, not a replacement for testing generated terrain.

The bounded budget is 12,000 updates at batch size 16, or 192,000 sampled map presentations. The preceding terrain smoke model used 2,000 updates at batch 8. The throughput probe used under 1.9 GiB of allocated GPU memory and settled near 12 updates/second after startup. Actual duration is written to the run report.

Checkpoints And Inspection

Run directory: data/models/terrain_joint_10km_1v1_long_v1.

  • progress.json: live update count, loss, elapsed time, throughput and ETA.
  • last.pt: atomically replaced model/EMA/optimizer checkpoint every 1,000 updates.
  • best.pt: checkpoint selected by full-validation EMA loss, not by cherry-picked pictures.
  • losses.json: training and validation history.
  • code_snapshot/, config.json: saved implementation, settings, runtime and data hashes.
  • samples/step_XXXXXX/: fresh-noise grids every 2,000 updates; the final grid has four seeds.

raw_height_water.png shows predicted height with predicted water overlaid. It does not discard the water prediction and infer all water from height. Raw unmirrored outputs are retained in every NPZ. The inspection grid applies 180-degree symmetry and records that intervention.

joint_projection.png is a separate decoder diagnostic. It combines learned height, water boundary and depth into one height field, with a feathered shoreline. It can improve geometric consistency without establishing that the model itself learned that consistency. The metrics therefore report raw height/water agreement, raw and projected pathability/connectivity, water-body counts and height corrections separately. No resources or textures are added and no playable maps are exported.

Windows Commands

$env:PYTHONPATH = 'src'
.venv-codex/Scripts/python.exe -m supmapai.tensors.terrain_joint `
  --source data/tensors/strategy_10km_1v1_v1 `
  --out data/tensors/terrain_joint_10km_1v1
.venv-codex/Scripts/python.exe -m supmapai.tools.audit_joint_terrain `
  --dataset data/tensors/terrain_joint_10km_1v1
.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m supmapai.ml.train_joint_terrain `
  --dataset data/tensors/terrain_joint_10km_1v1 `
  --out data/models/terrain_joint_10km_1v1_long_v1 `
  --max-steps 12000 --batch-size 16 --base-channels 48 `
  --eval-every 1000 --sample-every 2000

After an interrupted process has stopped, rerun the training command with --resume under the same configuration/code. Do not launch a second writer against an active run. The test split is prepared/audited but never used for fitting, checkpoint selection or sample comparisons.

More compute is an experiment, not a promise of map quality. We judge this run by water coherence, physical height/water agreement, requested-base suitability, connectivity, held-out reconstruction and diversity alongside denoising loss.
