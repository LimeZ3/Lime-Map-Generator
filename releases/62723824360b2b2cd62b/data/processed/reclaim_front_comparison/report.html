Separate reclaim and mex frontier layers

Original seven-map pilot plus four additional requested maps. Preserve the mex-only reference and inspect independent mex-only, reclaim-only and three context blends. No universal mass:mex exchange rate, new movement thresholds or route-count cutoff is introduced.

Weighting hypothesis

Let R be known initial reclaim stock, I the sum of configured T1 mex incomes and A playable area. Reference window T = sqrt(A) / existing land response speed (3.4 world units/s). The dimensionless stock/flow ratio is R/(I T); reclaim weight is R/(R+I T). All these fields are exported. The windows 0.5T, T and 2T expose sensitivity rather than pretending one window is established game truth. More mex income or a longer economic window lowers stock importance. Actual technology, collection rates and economy growth are unknown from the map alone.

For the land-front blend, substitute eligible reclaim on both actual sides for R. Full-map reclaim still appears in descriptors. Protected/rear/water/own-half-disconnected stock cannot attract a land battle route. Each objective is independently normalized by its own base-response cost; the combined access gain is (1-w) mex_gain + w reclaim_gain. This preserves meaningful per-layer responses and allocations at every weight.

Density

Known mass is retained per original world-unit cell, with no point cap or mass clipping. Dense piles directly carry more reclaim objective weight; distributed stock requires distributed contact positions. Gaussian density attraction uses the existing64-unit kernel, while response distances remain physical graph walks. This attraction kernel is spatial rather than geodesic, so nearby mass across a cliff can affect deployment preference but can never create a passable edge or shorten the scored walking response.

Report global mean density, mex density, effective occupied density area and concentration relative to uniform stock. Descriptor smoothing uses half mean mex spacing sqrt(A/N)/2. These describe local concentration without multiplying the same mass a second time into the global blend. Equal-total concentrated versus scattered reclaim can have equal global weight and different contact geometry.

Input and scope

Fresh numerical extraction uses the pinned existing blueprint index and FAF wreck rules, the same two active slots and default options. Live units are not valued as wreckage. Script-spawned future reclaim and regrowth are not simulated; unsupported rules remain explicit. Known missing blueprint expressions remain unknown, not zero. Blocked prop origins are projected onto legal terrain within5 world units of reclaim reach; the complete exclusion partition conserves every known mass unit. Each map exports raw, eligible-both and eligible-own mass NPZs and the original extraction entities/record.
