Natural Reclaim Values (naturalReclaimModifier)

Change mass & energy values of rock & tree props.

Effect support: natural_reclaim_multiplier

Choice Key Default / selected Initial mexes Initial hydros
300 percent 3 133 12

125 percent 1.25 133 12
100 percent 1 default, selected 133 12
75 percent 0.75 133 12
50 percent 0.5 133 12

  • 300 percent: Mass & energy values are 3 times higher.
  • 200 percent: Mass & energy values are 2 times higher.
  • 150 percent: Mass & energy values are 1.5 times higher.
  • 125 percent: Mass & energy values are 1.25 times higher.
  • 100 percent: Don't change the mass & energy values.
  • 75 percent: Mass & energy values are 0.75 times lower.
  • 50 percent: Mass & energy values are 0.5 times lower.
  • 25 percent: Mass & energy values are 0.25 times lower.
  • 0 percent: Remove Mass & energy values from rock & tree props.

Dynamic Spawn Of Resources (dynamic_spawn)

Determine which mexes & hydros should be spawned.

Effect support: supported_static_resource_rule

Choice Key Default / selected Initial mexes Initial hydros
mirror slots 1 default, selected 133 12
used slots 2 133 12
no mirror = no resources 3 133 12
  • mirror slots: Spawn resources for player & mirror slot (balanced resources).
  • used slots: Only spawn resources for player on used slots (unbalanced resources).
  • no mirror = no resources: Only spawn resources if mirror slot is also occupied by a player (not recommended, but it can make uneven matches fairer).
