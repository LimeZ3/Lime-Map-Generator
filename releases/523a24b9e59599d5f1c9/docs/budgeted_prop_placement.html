Budget-Controlled Learned Prop Placement

Change In Formulation

The previous twelve-channel diffusion pilot predicted count, mass, and energy independently and produced diffuse positive background with inflated integrals. This experiment separates cardinality from spatial placement:

  • Four explicit nonnegative integer budgets: trees, rocks, wrecks, other props.
  • A supervised U-Net predicts a normalized spatial PDF for each category.
  • The decoder samples exactly the requested number of points from that PDF.
  • A zero budget produces no points, regardless of the unused PDF head's output.
  • No procedural forests, radial reclaim templates, land-only mask, collision cleanup, or fallback positions substitute for model predictions.

This is a fast supervised placement baseline, not another diffusion model. Its neural field is deterministic for fixed conditioning. The decoding seed controls sampled positions and within-cell offsets, not a new neural layout. Independent point sampling does not yet model fine forest/rock relationships.

Exact counts are a decoder guarantee, not evidence of neural count accuracy. Generated examples average the full-map PDF with its 180-degree counterpart, then sample whole point pairs. Current symmetric budgets must be even. Sampling with replacement allows multiple entities per model cell. Fine offsets are decoded uniformly within the selected four-world-unit cell; these offsets were not predicted by the network. Coordinates use a 1/1024-world-unit grid.

Tensor Audit And Fix

data/tensors/prop_distributions_128_v1 preserves the full 1,020/97 family-separated split and uses original linear count fields, not previously clipped log targets. Raw 256-grid counts are sum-pooled to 128 and normalized per nonempty category. Terrain/base/resource conditions are mean-pooled using the same operation for training and inference. Profiles contain four train-normalized log-count controls plus water state. No per-map min/max scaling or count clipping is applied.

The audit found that the old log encoding capped count-density peaks and lost this fraction of count amplitude after decoding:

Category Overall Loss Worst Single Map Loss
Trees 5.48% 66.45%
Rocks 5.62% 52.62%
Wrecks 2.02% 24.31%
Other 2.62% 94.93%

These are losses in the old encoded training targets, not deleted source entities or the explanation for the entire previous generation overshoot. New PDF targets use all original count amplitude and preserve relative dense-patch weights. Previous tensors, source maps, and checkpoints remain unchanged.

Important remaining label limits:

  • A tree-group blueprint is one source entity, not one visible tree. Split group and individual-tree labels or retain explicit instance-based budget semantics.
  • The broad other class mixes non-reclaim decoration and valuable reclaim; split by blueprint role before claiming meaningful economic/style control.
  • Wreck sources include different prop/unit-wreck mechanisms; resolve them to concrete instantiable assets rather than treating all blueprint IDs identically.
  • Exact per-instance 3D scale and orientation are absent from current snapshots. Blueprint appearance data and yaw are retained, but exact reconstruction needs additional extraction. This is not a blocker for the present XY-placement test.
  • Counts remain known even when mass/energy is unresolved; the new placement loss does not replace unknown values with zero because it does not regress values.

Training And Evaluation

data/models/prop_distribution_128_pilot_v1 is a five-minute fresh training trial: 4,725,796 parameters, batch eight, bfloat16, EMA, spatial augmentation, and category-balanced spatial cross entropy on nonempty categories. Empty categories have no location target, and their zero-budget output is handled explicitly.

The trial completed at 3,658 updates with approximately 1,083 MB peak allocation. Held-out NLL is 9.101 nats versus 9.704 for a uniform PDF, a gain of 0.87 bits. This is a modest placement improvement, not a claim of competitive map quality. The source target entropies are substantially lower, so clustering remains weak.

It uses the approved terrain, symmetric learned 32-mex/two-hydro layout, and the medium seed-11 decoded prop points. Blueprint references and reclaim values are verified against the installed FAF env.nx2 archive. Unit instance scale is one; yaw is seeded and rotated with pairs. No texture model is used.

Resource footprint repair is bounded to 24 world units and is performed in whole symmetry pairs. In this example the largest move is 15.054 units, both starts remain fixed, and no water-class vertices change. Two underwater mexes remain underwater with flat footprints. Rounded smoothing feathers may overlap while protecting earlier flat cores; feathers stop at the original water-class boundary. The terrain-only land-connectivity check passes. The resulting verified-blueprint baseline is 2,832.16 mass and 2,872.8 energy, not values independently predicted by the placement network.

Native prop serialization writes positions, orientation basis, and scale into the .scmap prop section and independently reads it back. Four new tests cover prop round-trip/scale, bounded repair, coast-preserving underwater pads and paired removal without replacement; six existing native-export tests also pass.

Raw Diagnostic Export Is Now The Default

At the user's request, the active prop_playtest exporter no longer invokes resource terrain validation or repair. The former repair routine was removed from this active exporter; historical validated output is retained separately.

Current raw map: mapgen_sym_props_medium11_raw.v0001, displayed as MapGen RAW Medium 11, installed from data/exports/learned_props_medium11_raw_v1/manifest.json.

  • No mex/spawn/hydro snapping, relocation, flattening, footprint rejection, passability check, shoreline cleanup, or connectivity acceptance check.
  • No prop clearance, shoreline, or edge-margin filtering. All decoded in-bounds prop positions are retained: 1,024 trees and 256 rocks.
  • The original approved native terrain is unchanged, verified array-for-array against the saved terrain condition after .scmap read-back.
  • Marker Lua uses ten decimal places and bilinear grounding so fractional XY coordinates are serialized unchanged. All 36 markers' XY coordinates were independently parsed back and compared exactly with their source values.
  • Prop/marker heights are sampled from terrain, not used to accept/reject or move a point. Only finite-number, map-bounds, supported-asset and binary/Lua-format checks remain. Export metadata labels this format_only, not playable validation.
  • Existing symmetry/count decoding remains upstream. Raw here means unaltered decoded locations, not unconstrained diffusion noise or removal of prior terrain postprocessing. Starts are planned conditioning anchors, not neural predictions.
  • The game engine can still enforce its own rules; raw exports may deliberately contain unbuildable mexes/hydros, unsuitable starts, overlaps or underwater trees.
