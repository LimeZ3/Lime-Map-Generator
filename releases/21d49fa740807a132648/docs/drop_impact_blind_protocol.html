Blind four-map impact scoring: preregistered rules

Rules fixed before reading the four maps' drop predictions on 15 October 2026.

Scope: exact current local Adaptive Maridia, Adaptive Point of Reason, Adaptive Hilly Plateau and Adaptive Canis River; default adaptive options, only slot 1 and its 180-degree mirrored opponent active. Original known Astro/Seton's/Beetles outputs are retained as reference controls. No survey answers or desired four-map ordering are used.

Primary preset: medium for all maps. Base response strength: 1 for all maps. Provisional automatic land-route strength: clamp((mex share on the land component connecting both bases) × (straight base distance / shortest land route), .25, 1). This reflects how much economy the main land force can reach and how direct its approach is. It is a hypothesis, not a unit-allocation estimate or a complete calculation of naval dominance.

Score from 1 to 5: 1 + 4 × unique_affected_mex_share × (0.75 + 0.25 × (1 - exp(-distinct_destinations / 3))). Mex share dominates; additional destinations contribute at most 25% of the opportunity multiplier. One tiny target cannot score highly just because it exists. Scores are absolute rule-based numbers, not a relative min/max rescaling of these four maps.

Affected mexes are deduplicated across both attacking directions, with all active/default map mexes as denominator. One area on both sides is counted once. Destinations group the union of selected patches by walking connectivity/radius, rather than adding each side's counts. Expansion and offensive drop scores are separate; a combined drop score uses their union, not their sum. Gunship raid potential is an air-control-pressure proxy, not a score for all uses of air superiority.

Sensitivity: the same fixed 18 combinations for every map: three presets × route strength .25/.5/1 × base strength .5/1. Report score percentiles and pairwise rank consistency. These are scenario ranges, not statistical confidence intervals. The automatic route setting/medium/base1 is the primary display; no per-map preset is selected to maximize a score. Actual best settings require held-out community judgments and possibly replay validation.

Current economics and drop screens are unchanged, including hover engineers/water unloading and T1 resource assumptions. Map-level scores measure geographic opportunity, not actual initial unit/AA/intel placement, damage or win probability. Reclaim is not added to recurring mex-share value. Sequential-engineer travel, payload cost and naval fire support remain limitations from the existing pipeline.
