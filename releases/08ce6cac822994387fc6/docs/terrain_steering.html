10 km Terrain Steering Pilot

Historical checkpoint experiment: its economic front_width and route-style proxies are not the new terrain-only targets. See direct terrain controls v1 for resource-independent widths, bottleneck persistence, connectivity and fragmentation. Existing checkpoints have not been retrained with those controls.

Output: data/models/terrain_steering_10km_v1/. Four fresh seeds (1001-1004), seven profiles, 28 unique terrain samples. No game launch, playable-map installation, or retraining was performed.

The checkpoint is the existing full-map joint terrain model at update 12,000. All settings share fixed left/right requested bases, 180-degree symmetry, 100 initial DDIM steps, 50 symmetric refinement steps starting at t=250, guidance 1.5, and light native-grid smoothing. Refinement begins with averaged physical fields, re-encoded for the joint model. Unlike the earlier water-aware symmetry comparison, no rank-based water-area preservation is applied here. No water percentage is forcibly imposed, no passages carved, and no resource pads are added. Raw and refined model predictions are preserved separately.

Requests

All unchanged profile values use training-cohort medium presets. Only training manifest rows define relief presets and nearest-profile support diagnostics; validation/test rows are not used for this experiment.

Sweep Low / closed Baseline High / open
Water fraction 5% 20% 45%
Economic front-width control 11.17 31.60 60.45
Route-style proxy Narrow front + p80 relief Typical front + p50 relief Wide front + p20 relief

The route proxy is explicitly not an exact path-count request: the current checkpoint never received a path-count condition. Front labels are automated resource-dependent economic-front estimates, not human assessments. Geometric route width on terrain-only output is a diagnostic, not a recomputation of that same training label.

Initial Results

Water coverage is ordered low < medium < high for all four matched seeds. Mean final water coverages are 0.4%, 8.3% and 47.5%, respectively: clearly responsive, but not accurately calibrated. Raw model water coverages average 2.0%, 19.6% and 48.3%. The loss between raw and final water at the medium request shows that symmetry/refinement/decoding also affect calibration. This is not all a learned-conditioning failure.

The open-front setting has a wider shortest-route p10 width than the closed-front setting on all four seeds, but the visual differences are relatively subtle. Average geometric widths are 237 versus 190 world units. These are not the same quantity as the requested economic front-width label, and four samples do not establish reliability. One high-water sample disconnects the requested bases.

Relief responds strongly: the bottleneck-biased set averages 21.2 world units of p95-p05 relief, versus 6.3 for maneuver-biased outputs. This does not translate into dependable one-versus-many attack lanes. Small isolated hills can increase barrier-side route signatures without creating strategically distinct fronts. An open plain can have one route group and many valid attack directions.

PNGs and Measurements

water.png, front.png and approach_proxies.png show all four seeds without selection. Matching *_routes.png sheets add terrain-only route witnesses and grey blocked-land masks. Only up to six witnesses are drawn per sample; they are not certified attack lanes. Individual full-resolution images are under png/.

The existing raw movement masks, SciPy movement graph, bounded barrier-separated route sampler and route-width helper are reused. Routes obey eight-neighbour movement without corner cuts. No props/unit footprints are tested, route enumeration is not exhaustive, and invalid requested bases are not moved.

Profiles, checkpoint/source hashes, requested settings, raw/final fields, native heights, support distances, route witnesses and measured outcomes are retained in JSON/NPZ files. Generated README.md and summary.json summarize the run.

Windows Commands

Use a fresh output directory for new generation. No dependencies were installed; the existing CUDA and CPU scientific environments are used separately.

$env:PYTHONPATH = 'src'
.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m supmapai.tools.steer_terrain generate `
  --checkpoint data/models/terrain_joint_10km_1v1_long_v1/best.pt `
  --out data/models/terrain_steering_10km_v1 --seeds 1001 1002 1003 1004
.venv-codex/Scripts/python.exe -m supmapai.tools.steer_terrain report `
  --run data/models/terrain_steering_10km_v1

Generation took 53.6 seconds. Reporting is separate. Three focused tests cover profile isolation/train-only presets, blocked-base reporting, and route-witness behaviour on open terrain versus a central obstacle. These PNGs are structural previews, not engine screenshots or balance certifications.
