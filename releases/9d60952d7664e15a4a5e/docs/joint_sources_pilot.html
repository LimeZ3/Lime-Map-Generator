Joint source-layer diffusion pilot — 25 October 2026

Research closed, 25 October 2026. The final all-in report records the negative result, complete methods, compute and diagnostics. Earlier observations and instructions below retain their historical context; no training is scheduled.

The question is whether one sufficiently sized diffusion network can jointly learn the map's basic source layers and their association with gameplay ratings, without the staged pipeline's analytical channels and spatial constraints.

The local GPU pilot completed 2,074 updates and stopped for review before 04:02 UTC / 05:02 Amsterdam time. The reviewed twelve-hour continuation is now running in a separate folder. The selected generator checkpoints are not replaced.

First observations

The implementation check passed after 206 updates on 16 training maps: fixed-timestep mean loss fell from 1.02605 to 0.06942, and every channel improved. This is a small-subset learning check, not held-out evidence.

The separate, freshly initialized full pilot saved its first held-out validation and fresh-noise gallery at update 252. Held-out mean denoising loss fell from 1.01603 to 0.12379, with improvement in all 13 channels. Fresh outputs are still noisy and are not useful maps at this checkpoint: start density integrates to hundreds rather than one per channel, and height/depth agreement is poor. The raw failures remain in the saved arrays and galleries. Continue the unchanged pilot to the review point before judging whether more compute is helping.

Source releases and civilian heatmaps

data/tensors/civilians_curated_256_v1 contains all 1,117 curated maps (1,020 train / 97 validation). Its target is one float32 1 x 256 x 256 count-density array per map. Finite, in-canvas source civilian_unit and civilian_building records contribute one each; explicitly inactive records are omitted. Gaussian rasterization at sigma 1.5 cells conserves the entity count, including at edges. There are 24,968 included civilian entities on 217 maps (201 train / 16 validation). Zero means no identified source placement. Runtime/lobby activation is not resolved by this extraction. Building/unit IDs and footprints are not neural targets.

data/tensors/joint_sources_curated_256_v1 joins the corrected curated families and exact selected 10km 1v1 scenario. It contains 851 maps: 782 train and 69 validation, one representative per family. It excludes unsupported/incomplete RGB, unknown natural or wreck values, and incomplete rating vectors. Exclusion reasons overlap: 188 RGB, 100 wreck mass, five ratings, two natural mass/energy. The earlier 853-map candidate lost two more examples with unknown natural values. Known-zero wreck/civilian examples remain in the dataset. The original cohorts and source records remain intact; no held-out test manifest is used.

Exact model contract

One full-map float32 target 13 x 256 x 256, in this order:

  1. Terrain height, relative to the source height reference.
  2. Physical water depth, pooled from the native 512-square depth grid.
  3. Start A count-density heatmap.
  4. Start B count-density heatmap.
  5. Mex count-density heatmap.
  6. Hydro count-density heatmap.
  7. Absolute natural reclaim energy per cell.
  8. Absolute natural reclaim mass per cell.
  9. Absolute initial wreck mass per cell.
  10. Source civilian entity count-density heatmap.
  11. Surface red.
  12. Surface green.
  13. Surface blue.

Native height vertices are 513-square; cell heights are computed from their four corners and area-pooled to 256. Starts/mexes/hydros are rerasterized from exact source coordinates. Natural reclaim includes source props whose kinds are tree, rock, boulder or reclaim, including both mass and energy of those props. Wrecks remain separate. Reclaim sums use sigma-one Gaussian rasterization; these are source representation choices, without per-map budget normalization. RGB is the saved unlit material diagnostic: it omits decals, water rendering, props, lighting and normal-map shading.

Training uses reversible asinh height and log1p nonnegative quantities; scale factors use positive training-map samples only. RGB maps to [-1,1]. Source targets are not clipped and do not normalize each map to a shared amount. Every included map passed encode/decode round-trip checks (maximum relative error 7.07e-7), finite/shape checks and pinned source hashes. Physical arrays and encoded targets are both retained. Training caches encoded arrays as float16 on CPU, then converts batches to float32.

Generation inputs are 13 channels of Gaussian noise, timestep, and 12 ratings. Ratings map from the saved 1–5 scores to [-1,1]: expansion drop, offensive drop, air raid, drop impact, air mobility, land attack, naval attack, mex supply, reclaim abundance, early economy, front openness and army control demand. These are automated policy-specific scores. No clean source spatial channels enter generation. Starts are generated outputs. No SDF, pathing, front/access, economic analysis, eligibility masks, supplied anchors or validity-mask inputs are consumed.

Compute and training

  • One five-scale residual U-Net with attention at 32 and 16 cells, base width 128 and 73,323,149 parameters. All 13 output channels share the network.
  • RTX 5080, bfloat16 compute, float32 parameters/AdamW states, EMA, gradient clipping, aligned rotations/reflections and exchange of start channels.
  • Tested microbatch six with four accumulated microsteps: effective batch 24. Probe: 12,152 MiB peak allocated, 12,702 MiB reserved, about 20.3 maps/second. A batch-four probe was slightly slower at 19.7 maps/second.
  • The supervisor records actual GPU utilization, total memory use, power and temperature every 15 seconds. Early training samples were 94–98% utilization and approximately 15,000 MiB total device usage.
  • Objective: equal mean across 13 velocity-prediction losses, with additional emphasis on nonempty source entity/value cells. This target-dependent loss weighting is not a spatial input or an exclusion mask. There are no geometry, symmetry, water-consistency or placement auxiliary losses.
  • A 16-map, four-minute learning sanity check exercises positive civilian/wreck targets. This uses training data for both fitting and diagnostics; it is not held-out validation. Its weights are discarded before the full-cohort pilot.
  • The full-cohort pilot starts fresh if the sanity check passes: aggregate fixed timestep loss must decrease at least 20%, and every channel must improve.
  • Full pilot validation is on all 69 unseen families, at fixed noise/timestep, every five minutes. Resume checkpoints save at least every two minutes. Raw fresh-noise galleries and matched-noise mex-rating sweeps save every five minutes and at the end.
  • Learning rate warms up over 500 steps and remains constant during the pilot, so a later longer run can resume without inheriting an end-of-pilot decay. Training does not automatically continue overnight.

DDIM previews use 48 steps and classifier-free guidance 1.5. Clean estimates are numerically bounded inside sampling using training support; the unbounded final estimate and bounded sampler result are both saved. Displayed predictions and physical diagnostics use the unbounded final estimate, preserving negative-value or inconsistency failures. There is no symmetry projection, resource repair, count cap, geometric validator, budget composer or game-map export.

Use the hour review to check that every channel learns, whether fresh samples contain useful structure and sparse entities, and whether changing requested ratings changes the generated outputs. Denoising improvement alone is not proof of playable maps, calibrated rating controls, originality or competitive balance. One short run cannot establish that extra compute will or will not solve the task. The overnight run would test continued progress under a fixed representation.
