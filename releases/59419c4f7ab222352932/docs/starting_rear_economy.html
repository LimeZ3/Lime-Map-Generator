Fixed rules

  • Supported two-slot defaults and the selected meaningful mirrored opponent.
  • Near-base: at most 96 legal hover-path units from the supplied engineer.
  • Rear: outside that neighborhood, behind its spawn relative to the opponent.
  • Forward/flank: remaining own-side targets. Location is not a safety guarantee.
  • Separate access tags: ordinary own-half hover, requires crossing the frontier, transport required, shared neutral, unresolved or outside playable bounds. Hover-connected water mexes are included.
  • One engineer, one 300s clock and one cash balance per side. The mex-sized capital advance is disclosed and excluded from gain. No free completed mexes or ACU income. Mex costs/production, engineer speed and build time use the pinned FAF source already used by the project.
  • Greedy return per engineer-second, including walking, financing waits, building and 1 + mass/25 reclaim work. Completed mexes produce during subsequent work and movement. Partial final reclaim is supported.
  • Mexes require visiting their marker cell. Reclaim uses the existing nearest feasible hover-cell projection within five map units. Within five legal walking units of that access point, no further move is charged; otherwise the engineer walks to the access node. This conservative grid convention avoids introducing a new build-range or collection-route optimizer.
  • No energy, additional workers, upgrades, combat, scripted collisions or globally optimal planning. This is standardized mass potential, not an actual opening forecast or a calibrated 1–5 economic rating.

Operations are attributed to their resource's location. Mex income stays attributed there after the engineer moves. All location contributions sum to the one ledger; the categories do not each get an independent engineer budget. Neutral, frontier-crossing and transport-dependent stock remains in inventory and existing opportunity analysis, but is excluded from the ordinary own-half opening.

Terrain symmetry uses the conservative normalized mask. A plan is reflected only when matching physical resources, values and access nodes are verified; actual counterpart IDs replace the source IDs. Otherwise each side uses its actual inventory, with an explicit flag. The four examples have resource position or stock differences, so their opening plans are independently measured. This does not change the exactly mirrored battle-front geometry.
