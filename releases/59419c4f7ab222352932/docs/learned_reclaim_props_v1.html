Learned Reclaim And Props: Data Preparation

The initial milestone prepared source tensors, masks, an entity catalog, and diagnostic previews only, with training withheld at the user's request. Training was subsequently authorized; the full-cohort pilot is documented below. The historical rule_place_reclaim.py is not used. No texture tools, model weights, raw maps, or existing datasets are changed.

Representation

The intended learned stage receives nine spatial channels: the same seven terrain/base conditions used by the resource model, followed by the two exact resource heatmaps from the source map. Training inputs therefore contain terrain, planned bases, mexes, and hydros, not the reclaim targets themselves.

Twelve target channels preserve count, mass, and energy separately for:

  • Trees, including tree-group blueprints counted as source entities.
  • Rocks and boulders under the existing extracted rock classification.
  • Wrecks with the extracted reclaim values, not live civilian buildings.
  • Other physical props, including non-reclaim decoration with known zero values.

Inactive adaptive entities, live civilian units/buildings, and effects are excluded. Existing configuration-specific values are reused; game/map Lua is not executed. Out-of-canvas entities are counted in the audit, not folded back onto map edges. No land-only constraint or imposed symmetry is added to targets.

Targets use 256x256 grids, two world units per cell, and one-cell Gaussian smoothing with reflecting boundaries. Counts and known values are conserved within float32 precision. Exact unsmoothed totals remain in the manifest. Unknown mass and energy each have their own spatial loss masks; their blur support is also masked. Unknown values never become known zeros. Global amount controls have matching availability masks (12 values, 12 masks, water state).

Log-density scaling is fitted on training maps only, using a robust percentile of deterministic map-balanced positive-cell samples. Raw physical arrays remain available, including clipped outliers. Full-cohort preparation will refit this normalization on the full retained training split, not the pilot or validation.

Modeling Scope

After inspecting the typed targets, the full cohort was prepared and audited. The separate model uses masked losses and abundance controls. Sparse/dense values and count calibration must still be evaluated explicitly rather than assuming the resource pilot's foreground loss is appropriate. Training was held until the user's subsequent instruction to proceed.

Density prediction does not yet solve exact blueprint selection or instancing. Per-entity positions, yaw, values, blueprint references and available blueprint appearance/provenance are retained for that work. Full per-instance 3D orientation and scale were not retained in the current entity snapshots; re-extraction is needed if exact reconstruction of those fields becomes a target. A later decoder must use learned distributions and the asset catalog, not replace them with a rule-based layout. Resource-style symmetry and bounded collision/build-footprint validation should be explicit post-generation steps.

Full Cohort And Training Pilot

The full release is data/tensors/reclaim_props_curated_256_v1: 1,020 training maps, 97 validation maps, one map per curated family, and 6,222,820 in-bounds entities across 1,508 blueprint types. Unknown values occur in wrecks and other props: 1,307 wreck entities and 875 other entities have unresolved amounts. Their count targets remain usable; mass/energy loss support and global controls are masked separately. Ninety training maps and fourteen validation maps have at least one unavailable global amount control. None are silently relabeled zero.

All map tensor targets are prepared, but only 25 representative source previews are rendered to limit repeated image I/O. Normalization is refitted on the full training split. The original 25-map pilot remains immutable and separate.

A transient Windows permission error interrupted an unpublished progress-file update. New-stage JSON/checkpoint publication now retries sharing violations. The unpublished preparation directory was rebuilt with the same source contract; published datasets and original map files were not overwritten.

The separate five-scale ReclaimUNet has 5,010,412 parameters. It predicts twelve channels conditioned on nine spatial fields and 25 profile coordinates. Training uses masked per-channel velocity MSE without the mex pilot's target-dependent foreground weighting. Per-channel and foreground reconstruction diagnostics are reported separately so blank backgrounds cannot be the only evaluation signal.

Pilot configuration: 600 seconds, microbatch four, accumulation two, bfloat16, EMA, periodic held-out validation and atomic checkpoints. Source-code snapshots, dataset hashes and RNG state are saved. pause.request requests a checkpointed pause; --resume continues a matching unfinished run within its original budget. The loader rejects pilot_ready datasets and has a bounded RAM cache. There is no silent CPU fallback, and CUDA memory headroom/allocation guards are enabled.

Model progress is in the run's progress.json. The immutable dataset artifact's training_started: false describes the preparation tool only, not current model activity. All 59 reclaim-related tests pass before this pilot, including loss masking, augmentation, density/control decoding and Windows I/O retry behavior.

The diagnostic sampler uses the approved seed-303 terrain and symmetric learned seed-22 medium resource positions. Resource positions are re-rasterized with the training heatmap convention. Low/medium/high amount profiles come from actual, fully known training examples near the 20th/50th/80th total-mass percentiles, preserving real category/count/value combinations rather than inventing them. These profiles change category composition as well as total amount; they are not a one-variable causal test of reclaim abundance. It also samples fixed randomly selected held-out maps and shows source targets. Generated density symmetry is enforced in physical space, preserving each channel's sum. No land-only mask, amount calibration, threshold cleanup, or procedural prop placement hides raw model behavior. These are density outputs, not exported game props, and their integrals are soft counts/amounts.

Completed First Training Run

data/models/reclaim_props_curated_256_pilot_v1 completed at 1,465 updates in approximately ten minutes. The best EMA checkpoint is best.pt; the resumable optimizer/model checkpoint is last.pt. Peak training allocation was 2,365 MB. Held-out velocity loss decreased from 1.0771 to 0.0920. This is a denoising metric, not proof of useful generated prop layouts or economic calibration.

Gallery: data/models/reclaim_props_curated_256_results_v1/index.html. It contains six generated examples (two noise seeds, three amount/composition profiles) and three held-out examples with source targets. All nine archives contain finite arrays; all twelve PNGs decode correctly. Generated symmetry is exact and preserves raw per-channel sums. No props were instantiated or exported.

The model is not ready for game instancing. Raw output has diffuse positive background that produces large integrated amounts in sparse or empty categories. For resource seed 11 with the low profile, requested entity counts are 488 trees / 218 rocks / 16 wrecks / eight other props, while predicted density integrals are approximately 7,346 / 7,284 / 3,409 / 9,894. These are soft density totals, not actual spawned entities. The medium profile requests zero trees and zero wrecks, but the prediction still has substantial positive density for both. Tables in the gallery expose the mismatch rather than calibrating it away.

This establishes a functioning separate training/sampling stage and exposes a generation-quality failure. It does not establish that more compute alone will fix it. Next experiments should diagnose background/occupancy prediction and per-category amount calibration, then compare longer training with appropriate generation metrics. Do not replace the learned layout with procedural reclaim placement to make this pilot appear successful. Training and sampling are stopped; no further run or game launch is scheduled.
