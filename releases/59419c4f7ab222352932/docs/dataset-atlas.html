Dataset audit in the project atlas

The atlas Datasets → 1x1, 10km tab starts with the exact frozen collection of

the local dataset reviewer: terrain_full256_controls_v3, 1,200 train and 110

validation maps. No held-out test set is included. Its membership comes from

the published, hash-verified release, never from a newly inferred map filter.

The collection identity is the SHA-256 of that release manifest. The reviewer's

catalog and decisions must match it before a snapshot can be built.

This is the original audit population, including locally excluded maps. The

current curated terrain membership is displayed separately and can be filtered.

Local keep/exclude decisions and reason codes are a snapshot; private local

notes and undo history are not published. Atlas flags and notes are separate,

shared owner-edited judgements. Neither mechanism silently rewrites a dataset.

Views and evidence

The gallery can compare one selected source layer, derived tensor or training

channel across maps. The coverage table, search, split/curation/membership

filters and sorting work across the entire collection, with 24 maps per page.

Open a map to toggle source, derived, training, ratings and question panels.

Previews are rendered from saved pinned arrays and entity records, not predicted

data or new extraction. Native and Full256 target archive checksums are verified.

Each tensor retains its array field, source artifact, original shape, dtype,

finite check and numeric range. The display derivatives are 256px lossless WebP

sheets; they are not raw tensor downloads or training data. Height contrast is

map-relative, depth uses a 0–30-world-unit color scale, reclaim uses a log scale

and per-map positive-cell contrast. Natural reclaim bins sum 2×2 world units.

Sparse point/mask layers remain transparent over independently dimmable context.

Saved native physical channels are source-analysis representations. Their presence

in this audit does not imply every selected generator consumes them. Supervised

target channels are explicitly named; Controls & data use remains the authority

for actual model inputs, targets, masks and composer settings.

The full-bright preview uses the FAF vault URL associated with the exact version.

If unavailable, it falls back explicitly to the embedded author preview used by

the local audit app. Neither is a fresh engine render or evidence of alignment.

Saved source preview fallbacks and tensor previews are bundled in the snapshot.

Air masks preserve both actual selected slot names; no opposing mask is guessed

by rotation. Native resource-area IDs are identified as resource grouping, not

walking components. Only recognized saved route polylines can become battle-path

previews; scalar front metrics never become invented paths. Hover/navy movement

masks are not hover/navy battle-path predictions.

Ratings separate saved algorithmic 1–5 strategy labels (with confidence and

availability masks), measured terrain/economy values and FAF vault community

scores/review counts. None of those is competitive-quality certification.

Missing data, valid zero-valued layers, unsupported previews and exclusion from

a role-specific release are distinct. Check the processed-release membership

panel before treating an unavailable wreck-positive or other role target as a

failed extraction. Adaptive script uncertainty and provisional resource zoning

remain visible questions. Known work stays in map-extraction-todos.md.

Reproduce and maintain


$env:PYTHONPATH = 'src'

.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m supmapai.tools.build_atlas_datasets --workers 4

.venv/Scripts/python.exe tools/finalize_atlas_dataset_index.py

This reads existing artifacts, validates identities and renders audit previews.

It does not train, download, re-extract, mutate curation decisions, change splits

or approve maps. Run it after relevant recorded extraction/processing changes,

then validate and follow atlas-publication.md to publish the GitHub snapshot.

The builder is src/supmapai/tools/build_atlas_datasets.py, the UI is

docs/model-atlas/datasets.js, and generated content lives in

docs/model-atlas/generated/datasets/1x1-10km. Stable per-map atlas review keys

are dataset:1x1-10km:<map_key>. Dataset registration is recorded in

docs/model-atlas/datasets.json.

Future large-map, naval, team-game, survival and co-op collections should get

their own ID, explicit membership/scenario contract, split policy and source

receipts. Do not append them to this frozen 1v1 collection or reuse its review

keys when membership semantics change.

Experimental terrain symmetry sidecar

The derived-data panel now includes terrain-first symmetry from the pinned

1,310-map audit cohort. It shows family/order, axes, center, fit/domain errors,

separate source-start/default-mex agreement, selected-pair checks and the saved

28-value condition. The panel refuses a mismatched cohort identity. Results are

experimental analysis; existing selected model inputs and membership are unchanged.

See terrain_symmetry_detection.md. Regenerate/validate the small sidecar with

tools/finalize_terrain_symmetry.py --run data/processed/terrain_symmetry_collection_v2

using PYTHONPATH=src; this does not rerender the collection or train a model.
