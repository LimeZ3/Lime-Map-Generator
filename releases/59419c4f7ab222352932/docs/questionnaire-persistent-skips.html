Participant skip preferences

Skipping a map defers it until eligible unskipped maps are exhausted. This applies before the popularity/coverage alternation, so a skipped popular map cannot return just because the next invitation is from the popular pool. Deferred maps return in skip order; skipping one again moves it to the end.

Preferences are stored in browser localStorage, keyed by respondent ID. They survive review submission, category changes and reloads. QA preferences have a separate storage key. They do not sync across devices and clearing browser data removes them. Calibration anchors still require a partial review to continue. Manual map selection and Previous map remain available.

The Suggested maps collection also places skipped maps after other maps. Explicit popularity categories retain their popularity order.

Validation: survey and EN/RU checks, production build, and a browser QA skip from Zeuthea Valley to Adaptive Argon. Reload and category changes retained Adaptive Argon instead of re-suggesting the skipped map. Queue tests cover participant isolation, skipped popular vs fresh coverage, review submission, malformed storage, exhaustion and repeat-skip rotation.
