Route widths, shared sections, areas and resources

Implemented on the cached Archsimkats Valley routes on 14 October 2026. Preview: http://127.0.0.1:5177/navigation_corridors/index.html. The route polylines and IDs are retained; no new corridor discovery, map extraction or naval target calculation is needed.

Follow-up: symmetry-normalized influence and explicit merged sections are now implemented; see docs/symmetric_merged_routes.md. The raw result described below is preserved in routes_before_normalization.json. The current viewer uses the new normalized geometry and recalculated metrics.

Width

Each route-cell centre casts two perpendicular rays using a smoothed local route direction. Grid traversal stops exactly at the first blocked-cell interface or map boundary. Blocked side cells stop diagonal corner crossings too. The sum of both ray distances is the cross-section width, not twice the clearance of a line that might hug one wall. Cuts in the previous stage use nearest-side boundaries, which may be oblique; their width need not equal the perpendicular ray width.

Export the complete width profile, interior minimum and location, length-weighted mean, p10, median and an eight-unit sustained minimum. The sustained minimum is the minimum moving mean on a uniformly resampled one-unit arc-length profile. Exclude 32 units at both ends from summary widths; short paths use all samples. The viewer has individual profile plots and minimum cross-section highlights.

These measure the representative line, not the widest possible army route. Amphibious route 7 has a one-unit minimum at a turn versus a sustained minimum of 29.4. This is flagged as a sharp dip; it must not be promoted to an unavoidable one-unit army bottleneck. Width optimization/centering within the corridor and unit-footprint checks remain separate work.

Route territories and sharing

Compute a multi-source movement distance from every cell of each route across the original layer's eight-neighbor graph, preserving diagonal restrictions. Each reachable map cell belongs to every route whose distance is within eight units of the nearest route. The CLI parameter --shared-tolerance exposes this assumption. Disconnected terrain is unassigned. No distances leak through cliffs.

Export unique, shared and inclusive area, plus fractionally allocated area: divide a shared tile equally among its routes. Allocated areas sum to assigned terrain area and can be compared without double counting. Units are square map units, not a claim about controlled territory. PNG colors identify route IDs; hatching marks shared cells. Checkboxes highlight selected routes without reassigning the precomputed territories.

Pair metrics include exact shared edge length and fractions, lengths within the eight-unit proximity tolerance, length-weighted mean nearest-route walking distance, p90/maximum separation and shared territory area. Separation summaries exclude the same 32-unit endpoint buffers; near-shared lengths include endpoints. Exact sharing and proximity sharing are deliberately separate.

Default resources

Use resolved active default resources from the adaptive configuration, or explicitly label fallback saved-marker potential. Archsimkats has 54 active mex sites and six hydros in this all-slots reference. Assign each marker to the route territories at its stored position. A site outside reachable terrain is retained as unassigned; there is no silent teleport/snap across an obstacle.

Count unique/shared mexes and hydros separately and retain names and per-site route IDs. This is access proximity for the selected spawn pair, not economic ownership by a player/team. Other scenario slots and their resources remain in the all-slots reference. Reclaim-value association is not part of this milestone.

Why amphibious routes 6 and 7 differ

Spawns rotate into one another at 180°, but the stored movement masks are not exactly symmetric: land differs at 6,318 cells (2.41%); amphibious differs at 6,408 (2.44%); hover differs at 6,340 (2.42%). Mirroring route 6 onto route 7's side puts 45 route centres on blocked cells. The exact rotation also fails legal-step validation. The source height vertices themselves differ under rotation (maximum 3.5078125 height units; mean absolute difference about 0.0833).

The two outer routes have lengths 609.47 and 609.71, differing by 0.24 map units. Geometry also depends on shortest-path ties and the order of temporary cuts. The audit identifies counterpart barrier signatures and exports mirror validity, blocked-cell counts and maximum mirror deviation. Routes are not forced into an invalid symmetric polyline. This is a diagnostic, not proof of gameplay imbalance.

On the amphibious/hover view, route 6 has the largest allocated area (~70,991 square units), and route 7 is the longest. Route 6 has 13 exclusive and 19 shared mex associations; route 7 has eight exclusive and 19 shared. Shared site counts must not be added across routes as if they were separate resources.

Adaptive-resource export correction

The old strategic tool tested resource_type == 'mex', while the adaptive resolver writes 'mass'. The tool now accepts both. This explains empty adaptive mex lists in eight archived naval exports. They are listed in data/processed/strategic_features_featured/known_resource_issues.json, and the old audit is marked invalidated. The independent audit now checks the expected adaptive target set so this cannot pass unnoticed again.

No historical naval result was recomputed, in line with the request to avoid more per-unit calculations. Current route-resource associations read the resolved configuration directly and are unaffected by those stale target lists.

Files and checks

routes.json contains per-route widths/territories, pair metrics, resource IDs, rankings and symmetry diagnostics. territories.npz stores membership bitsets. The *_areas.png files are transparent display overlays. The *_ownership.png files encode bitsets in RGB for the viewer and are not intended as visible previews. The width.png files provide width profiles. area_comparison.png is the three-layer contact sheet. Geometry, input files and code are SHA256-pinned.
